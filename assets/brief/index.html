01 / Thesis

Price the pipeline, not the snapshot.

The directory is the visible output. The asset is an operator plus a production system: one person, running a disciplined AI fleet, doing the work of a data team — in languages and markets the incumbents have decided are not worth hand-collecting.

I will say the uncomfortable version plainly, because it is the actual investment case. I am the product. What is being funded is a repeatable conversion of domain access into structured data, and the three things that make it work do not come apart:

Access

  • Practitioners, temple communities, and dealers talk to me — as a sincere participant, over years, not as a researcher passing through

Taxonomy

  • A career spent turning folksonomy into curated ontology. The categories here are emic and contested; getting them right is the hard part, not the crawling

Throughput

  • A fleet discipline that turns that judgment into shipped, verified, edge-served data at a rate that historically took a team

Money buys the second and third of those faster. It cannot buy the first, and neither can a better-funded competitor — which is the whole reason this is worth looking at. Mot Dang is the flagship proof that the combination works. It is not the only output, and it is not the ceiling.

03 / The moat

What is actually owned

Being precise here matters more than being impressive, because diligence will check it.

Owned outright

  • The curated field data — the part collected on foot, not crawled
  • The Thai-canonical taxonomy and facet schema
  • The build system, importers, and deploy discipline
  • The brand, the illustrations, the offline app

Upstream, with licenses

  • The crawled fraction — OpenStreetMap under ODbL
  • Commons photography, credited per image

The defensible layer is the owned one: field truth folded through a curated Thai taxonomy. That is the part Google cannot crawl and will not hand-collect for a secondary Thai city — not because it is hard, but because the unit economics of doing it their way never close.

04 / The system

Why one person can hold this much

Build discipline

  • A route registry is the single source of truth; the build fails if data and navigation drift apart
  • Verification gates run before any publish
  • Edge-served from object storage — no server to babysit

Fleet, not chat

  • A tiered pipeline of agents with lanes, locks, and a timed push queue
  • Crawl → extract → taxonomize → verify → publish, each stage inspectable
  • Findings propagate across projects rather than dying in a transcript

This is the part that generalizes. The same spine has produced a second directory in an unrelated country and domain, and a 7,000-document searchable manuscript corpus with multilingual semantic search. The city is the demo; the factory is the company.

The corpus, and the register that sits on it

The same taxonomy engine, pointed at Thai sacred material. It is the part of the portfolio people react to first — and the part where the incumbents are furthest from being able to compete, because the categories are emic, the sources are in Northern Thai and Pali, and the market runs on provenance rather than SKUs.

The open layer

  • Multilingual semantic search over the corpus — the English-only embedding models are anti-correlated on Thai, so this required its own stack
  • Open by default. The scholarship is not the product and will not be paywalled.

The commercial layer

  • A register of temple, series, mould, and material — the four things a buyer actually authenticates on
  • Dealers pay to claim and maintain their entries. Money never moves through the register.
  • Sellers are already arriving through a live intake door; there is not yet a register for them to pay into. That is the build.

Boundaries, stated up front: no prai material, no tiger, no ivory — permanently excluded, not a policy that flexes for volume. A living tradition treated as living, never as curiosities for tourists. These constraints narrow the market and are not negotiable; they are also why practitioners talk to me at all, which is the only reason the data exists.

08 / Candor

What a skeptic pokes first

Stated plainly, because diligence surfaces all of it eventually, and it is worth more coming from me first.

Zero external revenue to date.

The sponsor system is built and live; nobody has been sold to yet. There is no revenue history to extrapolate from, and I am not going to draw a curve out of nothing.

Distribution was destroyed, not earned away.

A twenty-year audience on Facebook and Instagram was suspended in July 2026 with no recourse. The rebuild is an owned mailing list and LINE, days old. Acquisition is the genuine open problem, not the product.

Beeline's moat is demonstrated, not finished.

The funder-to-recipient edge is proven for 23 of 757 funders, from one of roughly fifteen annual filing batches. The method holds; the coverage is early.

Key-person risk is total — and I am not going to pretend otherwise.

If the thesis is that I am the product, then the concentration risk is the thesis, not a footnote to it. What is written down and reproducible is the pipeline: the build system, the taxonomy, the verification gates. What is not transferable is the access, and no amount of documentation makes it so. An investor who cannot hold that should pass, and I would rather they pass early.

The field-verified fraction is smaller than the crawled one.

"Approaching shoe leather" is the honest phrase. It is not there yet. Closing that gap is the single largest line in the use of funds.

The register with paying demand is the one I have not built.

Dealers are arriving at an intake door that leads to a page saying the register is still being made. That is a real conversion loss happening now, and the ordering of work is a fair thing to question.

The constraints on the sacred material cost money on purpose.

The excluded categories are among the higher-margin ones in that trade. I will not carry them. If an investor needs that revenue in the model, we are the wrong fit, and it is better to find that out on this page than in month nine.

09 / The ask

What the money buys — and an open question

There is deliberately no number on this page.

What the money does is settled — the table below is costed and defensible line by line. How much to raise, and on what instrument, is not. Naming a figure to look decisive would be the wrong instinct, and the wrong instinct is an expensive thing to hide in a first conversation.

So the ask is a conversation rather than a figure: what would this be worth raising against, and what would need to be true before a first cheque? "Not yet — come back when the sponsor line has revenue in it" is a legitimate answer, and a useful one.
