WHAT IS NOT COVERED BY EITHER, AND CARRIES ITS OWN

  A record names its sources in `sources[]`, and a field can name its own in
  `provenance` — source, how it was known, and when. What follows says which
  licence each of those source keys is under. A field with no entry has unknown
  provenance, which is not the same as ours.

  OpenStreetMap-derived fields — source type `osm`
      © OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database Licence 1.0 (share-alike)
      https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
      Held via Overpass. The API answers `osmDerivedRows: ODbL-1.0` for these.

  Overture Maps Places — source type `directory`
      Community Data Licence Agreement, Permissive, version 2.0
      https://cdla.dev/permissive-2-0/
      Release 2026-08-19.0, taken from the public S3 distribution.

  Thai government open data — source types `register` and `opendata`
      DGA Open Government License, version 1.0 of 10 June 2021
      https://data.go.th/
      Drawn from datasets on data.go.th: ONAB's temple register, OBEC's school
      register, the Ministry of Education's private-school licence register,
      CITIZENinfo, and others. The Terms prescribe a blanket reference where a
      producing agency cannot be identified individually:
          "The right to Use the Data is subject to the terms and conditions
          for DGA Open Government License."
      Attribution-only; no share-alike condition.

  Administrative boundaries — data/admin_boundaries.json
      Creative Commons Attribution for Intergovernmental Organisations
      (CC BY-IGO)
      Attribution string:
          "Administrative boundaries: OCHA COD-AB Thailand,
          Royal Thai Survey Department, CC BY-IGO"
      Via the HDX record data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-tha.

  The basemap — assets/tiles/cm-cr.pmtiles, served from object storage
      Built with Protomaps from OpenStreetMap, so ODbL applies to it as above.
      The archive is not in this repository; it is over GitHub's file limit.

  Photographs
      Each picture carries its own author and licence in its record and on the
      page that shows it — CC0, public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA, or a rights
      statement from the holding institution. None of them is relicensed here.
      The wat photographs came through mueang-map from Wikimedia Commons.

  The คาถา in data/katha.json
      Pali root: Mahāsaṅgīti Tipiṭaka. English: Bhante Sujato. Both via
      SuttaCentral bilara-data, CC0 1.0. The Thai script is derived by rule
      from the romanised Pali. The curated verses in data/curated/kathas.json
      are published everywhere in Thailand and are set down as tradition.

  Live tiles that quote an outside service — air, weather, water, finance,
  showtimes, lottery
      Each carries its source in the matching .meta.json beside it and its
      credit on the page that shows it. The figures belong to whoever
      published them and are quoted, not relicensed.
