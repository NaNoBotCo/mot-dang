# ตัดเสื้อ — the tailoring register (WO-49)

Asked by Nan, 2026-08-31, in three parts across the afternoon:

1. "Do a mot dang enrichment on men's and women's tailoring, including uniform
   tailoring, in Chiang Mai and Chiang Rai. Deep dive, don't come back
   emptyhanded. **Good for tourists doesn't equal good for locals or
   expats — different needs and expectations, all valid.**"
2. "Where do the high ranking police officers get tailoring done, is a question
   I'm curious about....."
3. "Also high quality western and thai evening wear tailors. Probably not found
   on Loi Kroh road."

Register: `data/curated/tailor.json`. Records: 50 into
`additions-chiang-mai.json`, 13 into `additions-chiang-rai.json`. Facet set
`tailor` (14 facets) into `data/facets.json`.

## What the measurement said

The site held **ten** records under `sub=tailor` in Chiang Mai and **zero** in
the whole of Chiang Rai. Of the ten:

* seven are farang-facing suit shops inside 600 m of the Night Bazaar
  (Ambassador, Unique, New Moda, Mr. Armani, You & Me, Universal, Siam
  Collection);
* one is a **shoe repair stall**, one is a **bootmaker** — neither cuts cloth;
* exactly one, `รับซ่อมแซม เสื้อผ้า`, is a Thai shop repairing clothes.

So the tailor shelf was not a tailoring register. It was a register of the
tourist trade with two cobblers filed by mistake, and it answered none of the
three questions above. This is the salon lesson and the notary lesson again:
`KNOWN_EMPTY` can be a fact about the search term, not about the city.

Read in Thai instead, the city is not thin at all. CMHY.city carries **126**
shops under ห้องเสื้อ-ตัดชุด, **119** under ร้านซ่อมผ้า-เครื่องแต่งกาย and **38**
under ร้านเช่าชุด — 243 of the 245 with their own published GPS, 174 with a
phone, 92 updated in 2025 or 2026. Chiang Rai's trade is on its own forum and
in Yellow Pages, in Thai, some of it a decade old.

## Four trades, one English word

The design decision, and it is Nan's rule turned into structure: a tourist
wanting a suit before Friday, a police major wanting a ชุดปกติขาว that will pass
inspection, a woman wanting a ชุดไทย for an ordination, and a long-stayer whose
only trousers have split are **not four grades of one customer**. Each strand
in the register carries its own words, its own prices and its own asks, and
none is offered as an answer to another's question.

## The geography is the finding

* **The tourist trade is one street.** 21 of the 126 sit inside 600 m of the
  Night Bazaar, and their names say who they are for — Hong Kong, Boston,
  Europe International, James Bond, Tony, VIP, Vincent Bespoke — English trade
  names written in Thai script, almost all carrying one tag, `#ตัดสูท`.
* **The uniform trade has no street.** Its 31 shops spread over ศรีภูมิ (8),
  ช้างเผือก (6), หายยา (6), วัดเกต (4), พระสิงห์ (3). They cluster around the
  **institution they dress**, not around each other. Six sit on ถนนสนามกีฬา,
  **38 m** from the National Sports University — and those six cut sports kit
  and embroider school badges.
* Chiang Rai says the rule out loud, in the shop's own name:
  **ร้านบอดี้โก๋ (หน้าค่ายฯ)** — "in front of the camp" — and ร้านโก๋,
  "opposite the camp's central gate, where they sell the vegetables."

## The police question, measured rather than repeated

The folk answer, and the only reply on the one Pantip thread that asks this
(`pantip.com/topic/34651186`), is **แถวๆ ศาลากลาง** — over by the provincial
hall. Measured against the 31 shops' own coordinates:

| anchor | median distance | within 2.5 km |
|---|---|---|
| new government centre, Chotana | **5.3 km** | **1 of 31** |
| old provincial hall, Three Kings | **1.6 km** | 22 of 31 |

The trade stayed in the old city and its northern fringe when the offices moved
out. The folk answer is not wrong so much as ambiguous — and read as the new
Sala Klang it would send a reader five kilometres wrong.

Chiang Mai is where the senior officers are, and that is structural:
**ตำรวจภูธรภาค 5** — Provincial Police Region 5, commanding eight northern
provinces including Chiang Rai — is at 311 Mahidol Road, Nong Hoi, 053 140 000,
under a lieutenant general. Even so **no uniform tailor sits at that gate**: the
nearest is 1.9 km away.

The shops that name the work in their own words:

* **ร้านเฟรนด์สูท**, Ratchaphakhinai Rd — `#ตัดชุดทหาร #ตัดชุดตำรวจ`. (Its own
  Facebook page: "ร้านเฟรนด์ตัดสูทเช่าจักรยาน" — cuts suits, rents bicycles.)
* **ร้านนิวบอย**, Chiang Mai–Lamphun Rd — `#ตัดชุดทุกเหล่าทับ`, every service.
* **เจ๊หล้าชุดและเครื่องหมายข้าราชการ** — "clothes AND insignia" in its own shop
  name, three branches. **This is the real answer.** For a senior officer the
  cloth is the easy half; the อินทรธนู, แพรแถบ, garuda buttons and name plate
  are the half that has to be right, and they normally come from a different
  shop entirely. In Chiang Rai that separate half is ร้านสตรองแมนโปลิศ and
  ณัฐพลเครื่องหมาย, next to the Teachers' Savings Cooperative.

**Live and dated:** on 19 March 2026 the RTP published a clarification that the
draft ministerial regulation catalogues 60 police uniforms (18 ordinary — 8
male, 10 female — plus 42 special) and that the khaki stays the royally-granted
khaki; reports of a colour change were, in its words, untrue. Comments closed
3 April 2026. A draft is not in force. Anyone about to spend 5,700 baht on a
bespoke uniform is entitled to know both halves of that.

## Evening wear — Nan's guess was right

Not on Loi Kroh, not on Chang Khlan. The trade is in **ห้องเสื้อ** — dressmakers'
houses — on residential streets: **วรมน** on Manee Nopparat (gowns, wedding,
bridesmaid), **ภรณ์อำไพ คอเลคชั่น** on Ratchawithi, **คุณแดงดีไซน์** at Nong Hoi,
**สแกนดิ คอลเลคชั่น** on Ratchamanka. For Thai formal: **สุพัณณดา** cuts in
ไหมแก้ว and sells the cloth, **ห้องเสื้อชุลี** on Arak works in ผ้าไหม and ผ้าฝ้าย,
**ขวัญกัญจน์** designs to order.

Two carry traditions of their own: **แสนหวี** on Sanam Kila Soi 2 cuts
**ชุดไทยใหญ่**, Shan dress; **แพรไหม** on Chotana Soi 6 sells ชุดไตย and Tai
books over the same counter.

And three nobody lists: **วีณา บราเซีย** on Anuban takes **custom brassiere**
orders — made-to-measure underwear barely exists as a listed trade and is the
hardest thing to buy here above Thai retail sizing; **โกแมว** on Nimman Soi 17
dyes cloth any colour (a faded black shirt is a dyeing job, not a tailoring
one); **บื๊กไซส์ชิกชิก** on Suan Dok repairs and sells big sizes at one counter.

## Rent or cut — the fork with numbers

`_pricesVerified: false` throughout; nothing here has been walked.

* **ร้านเดอะเกร็ท**, Nimman Soi 3: white suit **550 ฿** to hire (1,000 deposit),
  civil-service uniform **650 ฿** (1,500 deposit).
* A complete bespoke **ชุดปกติขาว**: one published breakdown came to **5,710 ฿**,
  of which ~3,300 was the tailoring and the rest insignia, shirt, tie, ribbon
  bars, buttons, cap.
* Ready-made khaki reported bought in Chiang Rai at **650 ฿**, and at
  **1,300 ฿** with cloth from a Chiang Kham market shop.
* **นำเทเลอร์ 1991**, Ratchaphakhinai — thirty years, its own graduation
  department: gowns cut from **9,000 ฿** on a **60-working-day** lead, Thai
  university gowns 1,200–3,000, overseas 9,000–15,000, CMU gowns for hire.

Chiang Mai has 38 rental houses. A directory that lists only tailors is telling
a resident to buy the expensive answer.

## The two asks Chiang Rai customers actually earned

From complaint posts on the local forum, turned into questions to ask **before**
the work — not into accusations against a named shop (the shop the poster named
is deliberately **not** listed):

* **ขอผ้าที่เหลือคืน** — ask for the leftover cloth back, and say so when you hand
  it over. One customer took in 4.50 m; a pair of trousers needs about a metre;
  2 m came back.
* **ขอให้เขียนวันนัดลงในใบรับของ** — get the finish date on the receipt. The other
  complaint is months of delay, jumped queues, and a sleeve finished in a
  different colour thread.

And the move locals recommend first, in their own Kam Mueang:
**ซื้อผ้างาม ๆ ไปหื้อร้านตัด (หื้อร้านคิดเฉพาะค่าแรง)** — buy good cloth yourself,
let the shop charge labour only. Kad Luang is where the cloth is.

## Open — what is NOT done

* **176 of the 245 CMHY shops have no place record yet.** The register carries
  all of them as data (name, tags, phone, GPS, the directory's own updated
  date); 50 were written up as records this pass. Not a silent cap — the rest
  are a next session's work, and the register is the work-list.
* **Chiang Rai has no pins at all.** Thirteen records, thirteen `needs-pin`.
  Getting the street and the phone means a call or a walk.
* **body-go.com is gone** — bot-verification wall where a shop used to be. The
  Yellow Pages listing still stands, so the shop is not assumed closed, but
  its phone is a lead, not a fact.
* **Two CMHY fetches failed** (ยาฮาดีไซน์, ฟรีสไตล์ 4x4), both in the repair
  category. Re-run to fill.
* **No prices verified.** Every number carries where it was read and when.
* **No shelf children added.** `tailor`, `alterations` and `fabric` already
  existed in `categories.json`; the four-trade distinction is carried by the
  new `tailor` facet set instead, which is the right instrument — the shelf is
  what kind of shop, the facet is whether it is worth walking to *that* one.
