CHANGING THE FUNDAMENTAL — from "has a counter" to "answers a question"
Mot Dang · architecture proposal · 2026-09-06 · nothing built
Companion to go/institutions (the problem). Counts from data/canonical + build.py as it stands.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
0. YOU ARE RIGHT, AND IT IS NOT A METAPHOR — IT IS THREE FUNCTIONS

  The counter rule is not an attitude I found in the data. It is written in
  build.py, in three gates, and each one is a line I can name:

  GATE 1 — build.py:8088  place_has_substance(r)
      "True if this listing offers a searcher something beyond a name pinned
       to a map — a phone, LINE, hours, a live site, a photo, or an address."
      Fail it and the page is noindex,follow.
      → 10,449 of 24,059 records (43%) fail it today.
      Every one of those seven tests is A WAY TO CONTACT A SELLER or a shop's
      own opening time. An archive whose holding and access rule we knew
      perfectly, with no phone, would be noindex. That is the rule stated
      as code.

  GATE 2 — build.py:6427  ANT_FIELDS, the nine ants
      nameTh · nameEn · phone · LINE · hours · website · photo ·
      owner-confirmed · recently-walked.
      4 of 9 are a contact channel for a customer.
      3 of 9 are a shop-shaped fact.
      2 of 9 (the names) are not commerce-bound.
      NO ant asks what a place HOLDS, who MAY USE it, on what TERMS, or
      whose BODY it belongs to. So an institution can never score above 2/9
      no matter how well we know it — and ant_rank feeds ranking and Gate 3.

  GATE 3 — build.py:6555  held(r)
      "A record with no pin and two facts or fewer: waiting, not published."
      → 1,836 records carry needs-pin; ~1,805 are held out of the ranked
      index entirely.
      "Two facts" is counted in ants, so it means two SHOP facts.

  Three gates, one rule, and the rule is: BE A BUSINESS OR BE QUIET.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. THE REPLACEMENT RULE

      A place is ADMITTED when a source lets us say it exists and what it
      is for.
      A place is PUBLISHED when the record ANSWERS A QUESTION SOMEONE COULD
      ASK OF THAT KIND OF PLACE.

  One rule became two, and that split is the whole architecture. Today
  admission and publication are the same test, and that test is a phone
  number. Separate them and you can be as inclusive as you like at the door
  without putting a single empty page in front of a reader.

  The second half is the load the phone number was carrying, done properly:
  "substance" stops meaning CAN I CONTACT IT and starts meaning DOES IT TELL
  ME SOMETHING I CAME TO FIND OUT. For a noodle stall that is still hours
  and a phone — nothing regresses. For a research library it is what it
  holds and whether I may walk in. For a mosque, which committee and when
  prayer is. For a foundation, what it does and for whom.

  place_has_substance(r) → place_answers_something(r), and what counts as an
  answer is read from the KIND of place, not from a fixed list of seven.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. FIVE CHANGES, IN DEPENDENCY ORDER

  ① A SECOND AXIS: kind[]  — what a place IS, socially.
     `cat` (28 keys) is a SHOPPING axis: food, hotel, cannabis, massage,
     repair, realestate. It is fine at its job and I would not touch it.
     Beside it, not instead of it:

        kind: commerce · institution · congregation · civic · school · wat ·
              infrastructure · land · memory · club

     A record can carry both (`cat: [sights]`, `kind: [institution]`). Nothing
     migrates; 24,059 records keep every field they have. The new axis is
     what makes a kind-aware substance test possible at all — you cannot ask
     "does this answer the question its kind invites" until the record says
     what kind it is.

  ② FIELDS THAT CAN HOLD A NON-COMMERCIAL FACT.
     Today the schema can say brand, licenceNo, cuisine, watCode, students,
     classrooms. It cannot say any of this:

        holds[]      what it keeps        — manuscripts, microfilm, a burial roll
        offers[]     what you can get     — (the tok-sen fix, already Phase 3)
        serves[]     who it is for        — public / members / researchers /
                                            a congregation / a nationality
        access{}     who may use it, on what terms, by appointment, fee,
                     languages, and an asOf DATE — mandatory, never inferred
        parent       the body behind it   — EFEO Paris, CCT, MFA of X, D3360
        ownWords     its own sentence, quoted and dated

     Per-field provenance already exists and already generalises (94% of
     records single-source, go/provenance). That is why this is safe: every
     new field arrives carrying its source and its date. The blocker was
     never trust. It was a fixed field list.

  ③ ANTS THAT COUNT THE RIGHT THINGS.
     The nine ants stay nine, but three of them become KIND-DEPENDENT slots.
     A commerce record's slots are phone / hours / photo, as now. An
     institution's are access / holds / parent. Same strip, same nine, same
     visual grammar — a reader still sees how well we know a place, and now
     an archive can be known WELL instead of known 2/9 forever.

     This is the change that stops the demotion. Gates 1 and 3 both read
     ants; fix the ants and both gates stop punishing the class by
     construction.

  ④ SOURCE GENRES AS FIRST-CLASS, EACH WITH A DENOMINATOR.
     source_register.json has 18 entries and all 18 are a map, a licence or
     a storefront. Add the genres, and add the property the crawl can never
     give:

        roster      — a list somebody maintains. THE denominator source.
        literature  — footnotes, gazettes, annual reports, bibliographies.
                      The only genre in which a HOLDING is ever stated.
        field       — you walked past; a reader told us. (10 records today.)
        inference   — a rule over what we already hold. (Proven: 73 records,
                      four shelves, go/shelves.)

     A roster hands you a number: "the consular list names N; we hold M."
     Absence becomes a measured defect instead of a silence. That is the
     silence-strain cure, and no amount of crawling produces it.

  ⑤ PUBLICATION WITHOUT DILUTION — generalise the escape hatch.
     The fear behind Gate 1 is real: admit 10,000 thin records and search
     turns to mush. But the codebase already solved this once. held() keeps
     a thin record out of the RANKED index while keeping its name in a small
     name-exact file, "so somebody who types its NAME is not answered with
     five other temples."

     That is the correct shape and it should be the general one:
        admit everything · rank by FITNESS FOR THE QUESTION ASKED ·
        let name-exact always win · never delete a record for being thin.
     A record that answers the asked question outranks a fat record that
     does not, whatever its ant count. Then inclusiveness costs nothing at
     the top of the results page, and Gate 1's blunt noindex can retire.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. WHAT MORE CRAWLING BUYS — AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

  You said you are generous with crawling. Where that spends well and where
  it burns, honestly:

  DOES NOT PAY: re-running Overpass. OSM answers "what did a mapper walk
  past". Another pass over Chiang Mai returns zero archives, zero mosques,
  zero EFEOs. Harder crawling of the same genre returns the same genre.

  PAYS, and these ARE crawls — of documents rather than of a map. Each is a
  candidate, each needs MEASURING before a line is written (the
  motdang-source-scout bot is exactly the vehicle):
     · DBD juristic-person registry — มูลนิธิ and สมาคม are REGISTERED, the
       way wats are. Already listed in source_register; never pulled. This
       is the single biggest one and it has a denominator.
     · MFA consular corps list — the honorary consuls.
     · Islamic Committee of Chiang Mai / Chiang Rai — the mosque roster.
     · Church of Christ in Thailand district rolls.
     · MHESI research units; the university institute directories.
     · National Archives branch list; the provincial culture office.
     · สกร./กศน. district learning centres.
     · Rotary D3360, Lions, and the clubs that publish their own rolls.
  I am not promising what any of these yields. Measure, then decide.

  THE MECHANISM BLOCKER, unchanged and measured: the keyless search endpoint
  serves a bot a CAPTCHA, and I will not dress a script as a browser to get
  past it. Facebook logged-out gave 0 of 5 known-good pages any fact beyond
  og:title. If you want volume through the discovery path, the cost is a
  keyed search API — Brave's free tier is 2,000 queries/month and is already
  sitting in source_register.json as an unfunded line. That is a real fork
  with a real price, and generosity is exactly what it needs.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. WHAT THIS COSTS AND WHAT COULD GO WRONG

  · The index grows. Today's is 6.2 MB and already a known weight problem
    (search.html fetches the whole thing). Admitting thousands more records
    makes that worse before Phase 4's work makes it better. Real, and it
    argues for doing ⑤ (fitness ranking) before the big roster pulls.
  · A kind-dependent ant strip is more code in the hottest, most-read part
    of build.py, and ant_bits is cached by id. Needs care, not courage.
  · An access rule without a date is a lie with a shelf life. asOf must be
    mandatory at the schema level, not by discipline.
  · Some of these rosters will be PDFs, some will be a page that has not
    changed since 2012, some will be behind a login. Expect a third of the
    list in §3 to yield nothing, and do not write the fill path as if all of
    them will land.
  · Inclusiveness has an edge nobody has drawn yet: does a bus shelter get a
    record? a shrine on a tree? a named soi? I think the answer is yes to all
    three and the cost is borne entirely by ranking — but that is a call, not
    a fact.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. THE DECISIONS — YOURS, NONE OF THEM MADE

  H. THE RULE ITSELF. Adopt "admitted if it exists and has a purpose;
     published if it answers a question of its kind"? Everything below hangs
     on this one.
  I. ORDER. My recommendation: ① kind[] → ③ ants → ⑤ fitness ranking →
     ② fields → ④ rosters. That fixes the machine before pouring records
     into it. The opposite order (rosters first) gets EFEO on the site this
     week and lets it sit there noindex and unranked.
  J. THE EDGE. Bus shelters, tree shrines, named sois, footbridges, the
     public wells — in or out?
  K. GATE 1's RETIREMENT. Does noindex go away entirely once fitness ranking
     lands, or stay as a floor? (It was put there for a real reason: a
     three-day-old domain's crawl budget.)
  L. THE KEYED SEARCH API. Fund Brave (free tier, then paid) or keep
     discovery hand-carried through the bots' own WebSearch?
  M. FIRST ROSTER. One roster, measured end to end, to prove the path before
     the other eight. DBD มูลนิธิ/สมาคม is my recommendation: registered,
     numbered, has a denominator, and yields hundreds of records in a class
     that currently holds 19.
