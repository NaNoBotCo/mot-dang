3. THE ROMANISATION CORPUS — did not beat translit.py, but FOUND BUGS
-------------------------------------------------------------------
pythainlp/thai-romanization-dataset, CC-BY-SA-3.0, 498,966 Thai->roman
word pairs, 27 MB. Downloaded and measured three ways.

FIRST, AS A REPLACEMENT — it loses, same as royin lost on 9/7.
On 1,500 records that carry both a Thai and an English name:

   translit.py                          243 / 1500   16.2%
   whole-name lookup in the corpus       37 / 1500    2.5%
   segment, then look up each word      192 / 1500   12.8%
   either translit or segment+lookup    249 / 1500   16.6%

The corpus adds SIX records per 1,500. Keep translit.py. (The 16.2%
here is not comparable to the 23% measured on 9/7 — the pool changed
when Overture folded in, and only 4,555 records now carry both names.)

SECOND, AS A YARDSTICK — this is the number nobody had:

   all pure-Thai words        147,229 compared, translit agrees 79.9%
   PLACE-NAME-shaped words     31,385 compared, translit agrees 86.5%
   words with ร์ + consonant    2,674 compared, translit agrees 46.8%

86.5% on the vocabulary our corpus is actually made of. The 46.8% class
is loanwords — Ford, mart, chart, Charles — which barely occur here.
So the general 79.9% understates it and should not be quoted.

THIRD, AS A BUG-FINDER — this is where the value was. FIXED SAME DAY.

MY FIRST DIAGNOSIS WAS WRONG and is corrected here. I wrote that the
misses were "ONE rule family: a cluster whose second member is ร or ล is
split". They were not. นคร, บพิตร, จันทร์, ศาสตร์ and อมร all read
correctly, so the cluster rules work. Two different things were broken.

1. A REAL RULE BUG — fixed in translit.py, `_wo_is_vowel`.
   กว/ขว/คว followed by ย is not a cluster: `-วย` is the rime /uai/.
   ก๋วยเตี๋ยว read "kwo tiao". 1,528 records carry ก๋วย. It is the same
   confusion that put แก้ว in the lexicon as Kaeo, one lookahead apart.
   Pinned against collateral damage: กวาง, ควาย, ขวา, ความ, กล้วย, ห้วย
   all still read correctly and the test says so.

2. SANSKRIT LOANS whose written vowel is not said where it is written.
   เกษตร is kṣetra, ka-set, not ke-set. No rule derives these from the
   letters, and data/curated/rtgs_lexicon.json is what this repo already
   has for exactly that. Ten entries added: เกษตร, เกษตรกร, การเกษตร,
   เกษม, เพชร, เพชรบุรี, เพชรเกษม, ถาวร, จอดรถ, ที่จอดรถ.
   739 records carried one. เพชรเกษม — a national highway — read
   "phetraketma" and now reads "Phetkasem".

A THIRD FIX WAS TRIED AND THROWN AWAY, which is the point of having a
corpus. Making loan clusters (ดร, บร, บล, ฟร, ฟล) form only at a word
start, to stop จอดรถ reading "chodrot". It did not fix จอดรถ — it moved
the error to "chodarot" — and it measured WORSE, -187 words across the
corpus. Reverted; จอดรถ went to the lexicon instead. A plausible rule
that measures worse is worth more written down than one never measured.

THE RESULT, same corpus, before and after:

    all pure-Thai words     79.90%  ->  80.86%   (+1,417 of 147,229)
    place-name-shaped       86.50%  ->  88.34%   (of 31,292)

TWO ARE DELIBERATELY NOT FIXED, with reasons in the test:
  พลวง  _rom_word gets it right; _segment splits พล|วง before the reader
        sees it, because พลวง is not in search_segdict.txt. A lexicon
        entry cannot reach it — segmentation runs first. 0 records.
  แหน   the corpus says "haen", we say "nae", and neither is simply
        right: แหน is two words, /nae/ with a silent leading ห (จอกแหน)
        and /haen/ where ห is the initial (หวงแหน). 44 records. Changing
        it trades one wrong answer for another.

NO URL MOVED. `place_slug` -> `_slug_stem` -> `name_of`, and name_of is
`name or nameEn or id` — it never calls translit. The romaniser feeds
readings on the page, not filenames, so there is no 404 risk in this
change. Checked before the edit, not assumed.

Tests: tests/test_translit_clusters.py (26 checks, rewritten to pin the
fixes AND the collateral), tests/test_translit.py and tests/test_terms.py
all pass. A build was running in another session throughout; build.py was
not run, so these readings reach the site on the next build.
