WHAT THE REGISTER DOES NOT CARRY

No coordinates.  No phone numbers.  No street addresses beyond the ตำบล.  No
room counts.  No dates.  Name and place is the whole of it, the same shape as
the weed.th harvest.  Nothing in the importer will ever fill contacts and the
docstring says so, so nobody goes hunting for a bug that is not there.

HOW MUCH OF THIS IS NEW — NOT KNOWN, AND SAYING SO

  register records ................................... 762
  exact name match anywhere in the CR corpus .......... 20
  match after naive RTGS romanisation ................. 49

Do not read 713 as "713 new places".  741 of the 762 register names are Thai
script; the corpus holds 487 CR lodging records and only 80 of them carry a
Thai name at all.  "Grace Hostel" in OpenStreetMap and เกรซ โฮสเทล in the
licence roll are one hotel, and romanisation does not recover it, because the
Thai is a transcription OF the English.  The overlap is genuinely unmeasured.

Both possible answers are worth having.  If most are new, the shelf grows.  If
most are already held, this is the Thai-name layer that 86% of CR lodging
lacks — which is the thing that makes a place findable by the people who live
here.  Reconciliation is its own job; reconcile_names.py exists for it and
ตำบล is the disambiguator.


CHIANG MAI — STILL OPEN

Chiang Mai publishes no named list.  Its provincial catalogue carries counts
only, and harvest_datagoth.py already holds them as yardsticks: 1,193
certified accommodations in อ.เมืองเชียงใหม่ alone (2567); 469 hotels, 339
resorts, 201 guesthouses in the older series.  Eight of every ten lodging
records we hold are Chiang Mai, so this is the gap that matters.

Three candidates, none cracked:

  thailandsha.com — TAT's SHA membership directory.  Live, answers 200,
  Next.js, noindex, no robots.txt, no sitemap.  A backend API exists and is
  findable in the JS chunks.  Not yet found.

  datawarehouse.dbd.go.th — answers 200.  Every licensed hotel is a juristic
  person; TSIC hotel codes filtered to Chiang Mai would give names and
  registered addresses.  Not yet probed.

  the affiliate's own feed — below.


THE AFFILIATE RAIL, WHICH IS ALSO A DATA SOURCE

Travelpayouts marker 749581.motdang is already in data/flights.json and
already live on the flights board, with the bilingual referral disclosure
written:

  ปุ่มเช็คราคาเป็นลิงก์แนะนำ (affiliate) — ราคาที่เห็นเท่าเดิมทุกบาท
  Fare buttons are referral links — the price you see is unchanged.

api.travelpayouts.com/data/en/hotels.json answers 403, not 404.  The hotel
dump exists and is gated on an API token — a different credential from the
marker, issued by the same account.  That is what makes lodging different from
every other shelf on the site: the partner who pays on a booking is also the
one who can hand over names, coordinates and stars for the Chiang Mai gap the
government will not fill.

THE THING THAT IS TRUE ABOUT THE MONEY

Nobody has paid anything to use motdang and the traffic is Nan, Claude and a
couple of friends (9/5).  Affiliate links earn on bookings.  This builds the
instrument; it does not conjure the readers.  Worth building anyway — the
instrument has to exist before any reader can use it — but nothing here should
be costed as revenue.
