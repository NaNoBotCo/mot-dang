================================================================================
1. WHAT /here.html DOES
================================================================================

One button: 📍 เริ่มจากตรงนี้ · Start where I am.  Then, in order:

WHERE YOU ARE
  "You are inside the moat — the old city" / "around Nimman" / "out of town,
  old city 9.2 km to your south-east ↘", then the nearest gate or corner as a
  local would say it: "Thapae Gate is 25 m to your north-east ↗", and the
  GPS accuracy (±12 m). Zones are data/curated/zones.json (the 10 ย่าน + 10
  amphoe circles). Gates are the 9 catalogue records the moat drawing uses.

WHAT IS NEARBY
  Every kind present within ~1 km, nearest 3 each (tap "more" for 10), in
  pavement order: food, wat, essentials, market, massage, medical, sights,
  shopping, parks, museums, hotels, transport ... Chips switch kinds off.
  Each row: name (ไทย · EN), sub-shelf (คาเฟ่ · Café), 🐜 rank, straight-line
  distance, an arrow for the direction, and — ONLY where the place posted
  hours — เปิดอยู่ / ปิดอยู่ (ตามเวลาที่แจ้ง). No hours = no claim.

NAVIGATE
  🧭 นำทาง — a geo: link, opens the phone's own map app with turn-by-turn
     (the same link near.js already uses on /chuai.html)
  🚶 วางแผนเดิน — plan.html?stops=cm:slug, our planner, walk vs ride
  ☎ the phone as written, tappable
  ⌖ กลับมาที่ฉัน — recentre the map on you
  The dot follows you (watchPosition) and the list re-ranks after 25 m.

SHARE
  🔗 ส่งจุดนี้ — here.html#lat/lng (4 dp, ~11 m). The ONLY link on the
     site that carries a position; the button says so. The recipient opens
     it and sees the same spot, labelled "this spot came from a link you
     were sent, not from your device", with the same nearby list.
  📷 ส่งภาพนี้ — the map as a picture with the caption and the ODbL credit
     in the pixels (unfurls in LINE where a link does not). Shown only when
     the browser can hand a file to another app (true on your vivo).
  Each place row links to its page, which has its own share block.

THE SWITCH
  ☐ เริ่มหาตำแหน่งทันทีเมื่อเปิดหน้านี้ · Start locating as soon as this
  page opens.  A preference (localStorage `md-here`), never a coordinate. It
  only acts once the browser already reports the permission GRANTED, so it
  can never pop the OS dialog by itself. Bookmark /here and it opens on you.

================================================================================
4. WHAT IT WILL NOT DO, ON PURPOSE
================================================================================

- Ask for your position on load. The site's rule (MDLOC in md.js) is one
  door, opened by a tap. The switch is the one exception and it cannot
  produce a dialog, only skip a tap you already took once.
- Write your position to the URL, to disk, or to us. tests/test_here.py
  fails the build if here.js ever calls replaceState/pushState or stores
  anything but the switch.
- Call anything "closed" that never posted hours.
- Quote walking minutes. Distances are straight lines and say so; the
  planner is one tap away for the real walk.
