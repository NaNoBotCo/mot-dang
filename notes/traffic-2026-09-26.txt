TRAFFIC  ·  รถติด  ·  motdang.net/traffic.html
Built local 2026-09-26. Not published.

WHAT IT DOES
- Opens on the reader's clock: "Saturday 20:00 · slow · a 10-minute drive takes 14".
- 24 bars for the day, 7 day buttons, a week grid. Tap any to move the forecast.
- Map: jam hexagons over central Chiang Mai at the hour picked. Highway lines
  coloured by the Department of Highways grade A to F; thicker = more vehicles.
  Tap a line: vehicles a day, motorcycles a day, grade.
- Beside the number: rain (Open-Meteo), Sunday Walking Street hours (OSM),
  festival days (the Mot Dang calendar).
- When waiting saves a minute: "Wait until 22:00 and it takes 13 instead of 14".
- Chiang Mai, Chiang Mai province, Chiang Rai buttons move the map.

NUMBERS
- Worst hour of the week: Friday 17:00, 10 minutes takes 19.
- Clearest hour 06:00 to 22:00: Sunday 06:00, 11.
- 129 highway sections in the two provinces. Busiest: route 1141, Mahidol Road,
  121,403 cars, buses and trucks a day plus 26,366 motorcycles, grade E
  (at capacity). Grade F (over capacity): 5 sections.

THE SUM
  congestion = TomTom's figure for that weekday and hour × that month ÷ the year
  minutes    = 10 × (1 + congestion ÷ 100)
Rain, festivals and walking streets show as lines; nothing on disk measures
what they do to the number.

SOURCES
- Department of Highways, open data: traffic count road lines 2568 (its map
  server, onemap-layers.doh.go.th) and congestion grades by section 2568.
  DOH's 2568 grade file has its province column scrambled: 98.5% of rows name
  the wrong province. The values match their rows (88% same grade as 2567, the
  same agreement 2566 had with 2567), so the join is on route and section.
- TomTom Traffic Index 2025, Chiang Mai page: congestion for every weekday
  hour, by month, and the jam hexagons. TomTom marks the page all rights
  reserved. Chiang Rai has no TomTom figure; the page says so.
