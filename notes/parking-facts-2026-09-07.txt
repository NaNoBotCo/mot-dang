1. THE ONE-LINE DIAGNOSIS
==============================================================================

Seven car parks stand within 250 m of Tha Phae Gate. The site answered her
question with one 810 m away, because it read "we do not know whether bikes
may park here" as "bikes may not park here."

  amenity=motorcycle_parking   132 records   ← the only thing the query saw
  amenity=parking            1,632 records   ← excluded
  of those 1,632, the number OSM says anything at all about motorcycles: 3
     (motorcycle=yes 2, motorcycle=no 1. capacity:motorcycle: 0.)

So 1,629 car parks are silent on the question, and the site turns that
silence into a no. That is the rule Nan already wrote for the Listing Sheet —
UNKNOWN IS NOT ZERO — broken here in the one place it costs a rider the
answer.

AND THE SILENCE IS NOT EVEN AMBIGUOUS. Nan, 2026-09-07: "In chiang mai,
there is no such thing as a 'car park' exclusively for cars." Every lot in
this city takes motorcycles. So the question the site is asking of each
record — may bikes park here — has one answer everywhere and did not need
asking; what it should have been carrying is the opposite fact, which lots
take BIKES ONLY. See fork A.

The 134 rows she got are the whole province's motorcycle bays sorted by
distance from the gate. It is a correct list of the wrong set.

==============================================================================
4. "HOW LONG MAY I STAY" IS NOT ONE OF THE QUESTIONS THE SITE THINKS A CAR
   PARK HAS TO ANSWER
==============================================================================

data/curated/kinds.json, the parking kind:

  say_en:  "where it is, whether it costs, whether you may use it,
            how many fit"
  ant_slots.parking: attrs.fee · attrs.access · attrs.capacity ·
                     attrs.parkingType

maxstay and motorcycle are in the kind's `fields` list — so the answer-set
admits them — but neither is an ant slot. The consequence is exact: the site
never asks anyone for a time limit, never shows it as missing, and could not
display it if somebody sent it in. Nan just demonstrated that for a rider at
a half-hour bay it is the FIRST question, ahead of capacity and ahead of
structure. A lot you may not leave the bike at for an hour is not the same
answer as a lot you may.

Same argument, weaker, for `motorcycle`: 5 records carry it.

HOW IT WAS GEOCODED, so the next person can redo or overturn it:

  · The results map centres on the resolved landmark — build.py:4360,
    map.jumpTo({center: pointNow, zoom: 15}) — and its box is 922 x 280 CSS
    px, read off the live page. Tha Phae Gate is 18.7877625, 98.9932697
    (docs/data/search_landmarks.json).
  · Her scale bar reads 50 m across ~9% of the map width. At MapLibre zoom 17
    the box spans ~520 m, which puts a 50 m bar at 9.6%. Zoom 15 puts it at
    2.4%. So she had zoomed two steps in, and the frame is ~520-540 m across.
  · The circle sits on the map's centre, radius ~40 m at that scale. It is
    marking an AREA — the gate plaza — not a point.
  · Inside that circle, on 2026 satellite imagery, the plaza's south-east
    apron carries dense rows of small units west of ถนนคชสาร. Scale
    calibrated empirically by shifting the map centre 0.00050 lng (52.7 m)
    and measuring the label shift: 410 px, so 0.1286 m/px. The units run
    ~15 px against ~35 px for the cars on the road alongside — 1.9 m against
    4.5 m. Two-wheelers, parked in rows.

  THE PIN: 18.78764, 98.99357 — 34 m ESE of the gate, inside her circle.
  APPROX, not exact: the painted extent has not been walked and the rows move
  when the plaza is used for events.
