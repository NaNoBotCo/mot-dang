================================================================================
1. THE DISEASE — FOUR STRAINS, ONE ORGANISM
================================================================================

Search disease = the gap between what a person ASKS and what an index HOLDS.
Every symptom we have seen since July is one of four strains.

STRAIN A — THE NAME STRAIN.  Indexes hold what a thing is CALLED, never
  what it OFFERS. A menu item, a technique, a service, a dialect word is
  invisible. Tok sen is the type case: sold in thirty shops, printed on
  zero signboards. Also: physio inside "clinic", ขัดขี้ไคล inside "spa",
  ล้างแอร์ refused for saying wash instead of mend.

STRAIN B — THE SOURCE STRAIN (the inverse-coverage law).  The closer to
  a practice's epicentre, the weaker the structured signal, because there
  it is ordinary and nobody writes it into a category. The categories that
  ship worldwide were written by outsiders for whom it was exotic. So the
  world capital of ตอกเส้น has the world's worst data about it. Our own
  version of this: we used a MAP (OSM, English-skewed) as a TRADE REGISTER.
  Massage shelf: 302 records, 294 from OSM, 21 with any Thai in the name.

STRAIN C — THE SCRIPT STRAIN.  The question arrives in one script
  ("toksen", "toke sen", "tok-sen"), the answer lives in another (ตอกเส้น),
  and nothing bridges them. Google bridges it with billions of documents;
  we must bridge it with a vocabulary we write on purpose.

STRAIN D — THE SILENCE STRAIN.  Nothing measures the miss. A shelf sits at
  zero for months and the only detector is a reader who leaves. Today the
  site's own search box records nothing, the AI's log holds my exams and
  almost no readers, and 18 of 34 empty shelves have no written reason.

The organism under all four: THE UNIT OF INDEXING IS THE NAME, AND THE
UNIT OF DEMAND IS THE NEED. Google closes that gap with scale. Mot Dang
closes it by being written from the inside — a category tree, a vocabulary
and a schema that say what a place DOES, in the words the city uses,
with a source on every claim. That is the only cure a one-person directory
can afford, and it is also the only one Google structurally cannot copy
(see /why.html: their data cannot be downloaded; ours can).

3. WHAT A CURED CITY LOOKS LIKE — FIVE LAYERS, EACH WITH A TEST
================================================================================

  L1  VOCABULARY — what people ASK.        Test: every home-ground term and
      every asked.json key has an entry with Thai, translits, gloss, shelf.
  L2  CORPUS — what EXISTS.                Test: for each term, held ≥ the
      order of magnitude an outside source says exists.
  L3  SCHEMA — what each place OFFERS.     Test: a record can say "does tok
      sen, per its own Facebook page, seen 2026-09-05" without lying about
      what it IS.
  L4  RETRIEVAL — one index, three doors.  Test: site search, /api/search
      and the AI answer the SAME 50 real questions, scored the same way.
  L5  LOOP — every miss is a work item.    Test: a zero-result query or an
      uncited AI search lands in a queue within a day; a zero shelf without
      a reason fails the build.

The discipline stays what AGENTS.md says: provenance on every fact, and no
inference about what a shop "really is".

================================================================================

  a. Land the tok sen harvest: 10 tags + 20 records incl. Phailin (go/toksen).

  Moves: tok-sen 0 → 30 · silent zero shelves 18 → 0 · the reader's path in

PHASE 1 — THE HOME-GROUND VOCABULARY                  (2 days)
  data/curated/vocabulary.json — one file, one entry per term for which
  Chiang Mai / Chiang Rai IS the authority, plus every asked.json key:
    { th, translits[] (incl. the misspellings readers type), en, shelf,
      offers-key, expected ("dozens" | "hundreds"), sources-to-query[] }
  Mine first: the 26 asked keys and the 198 Chiang Mai conversations in the
  ChatGPT archive (2.9). Then the home-ground terms, ~60: ตอกเส้น ย่ำขาง ขัดขี้ไคล ประคบ จับเส้น ราชสำนัก · สักยันต์

  กายภาพบำบัด กิจกรรมบำบัด ล้างแอร์ ถ่ายเอกสาร … and the 26 asked keys.
  ONE file drives FOUR consumers (all already exist, all hand-fed today):
    search-core thesaurus (site search) · index_labels.json (Vectorize
    text) · MOTDANG_TAGS (the AI planner) · the coverage test (Phase 5).
  Moves: Strain C closed for every term in the file; adding a term becomes
  data entry, not four code edits.

PHASE 2 — REBALANCE THE SOURCES: REGISTERS FIRST      (the real work · ~2 weeks,
                                                        one trade at a time)
  Stop using the map as the trade register. For every Thai-named trade, a
  Thai-native source, on the ONAB/CMHY pattern that is already proven here:
    · thdata.co — 337 CM massage with Thai names, addresses, phones (crawled

    · สบส. health-establishment licence register (สถานประกอบการเพื่อสุขภาพ,
      B.E. 2559) — the massage spine, and the licence axis the massage facet

    · DBD company register (browser fetch; 403s to scripts)
    · the 806 records that ALREADY carry a Facebook page — their own words,
      never crawled; the richest offers source we hold
    · cmhy job table extended to Chiang Rai (today CM only — 2.3)
    · Wongnai / GoWabi — FORK F3 below (terms of service)
  Every ingest: fetchlog ledger (verify-before-believe), dedupe by door AND
  trade, refusals written down and READ (a refusal list that reads like a
  trade directory is the tell — twenty-follow-ups note).
  Order: massage first (the type case, the harvest is half done), then the
  17 other empty-by-name shelves, then CR.
  Moves: Thai-named massage 7% → majority · CR curated 28 → hundreds ·
  Strain B addressed at the spine.

PHASE 3 — THE SCHEMA: `offers[]` AND `blurb`          (3 days)
  `sub` says what a place IS. Add what it DOES:
    offers: [ {k:"tok-sen", via:"own-facebook", seen:"2026-09-05", url:…} ]
  plus a short `blurb_th`/`blurb_en` in the shop's OWN words (quoted, dated,
  sourced — never written by us). Render offers as a menu row on the place
  page; fold offers + blurb into the Vectorize text and the site index.
  Evidence bar (FORK F4): own name, own site, own page, menu photo, licence
  = offers. A reader review = attrs only (the scrubglove precedent). A
  sidebar link = nothing (the 33-of-36 thdata trap).
  Migrate: shelves.json entries whose evidence is a menu, not a sign, become
  offers; the shelf keeps counting them.
  Moves: Strain A closed structurally; "who does X near me, open at 21:00"
  becomes a query the data can answer.

PHASE 4 — ONE INDEX, THREE DOORS                       (3 days)
  Site search (search-core JS), /api/search and the AI planner read the
  same vocabulary and the same offers. Put hours and area into the site
  index so open-now / ย่าน are filters, not shrugs (SEARCH.md's own "next
  real step"). Fix the two search-core gaps that bite shelves (compound
  segmentation via vocabulary; a filter word that is also a shelf word
  survives as a term).
  The eval: tests/answers.json — 50 real questions (asked.json + the AI
  log + your own), each with the record(s) that must appear. Scored against
  ALL THREE doors in one run. Today the AI has a 24-question exam and the
  site has a 26-query one, and they do not share a question.
  Moves: one scoreboard for the whole platform; a regression on any door
  is visible the same day.

PHASE 5 — THE LOOP AND THE NUMBER                      (2 days, then standing)
  a. /coverage.html — public, dated, unflattering on purpose: per
     vocabulary term, held vs expected vs the outside count. "Expected 20+,
     holding 0" printed where a reader can see it. This is 2.4 made into a
     page, and it is the same honesty-as-interface that reach.html already
     practises.
  b. Zero-result queries on the site search → a local, IP-free, session-
     free counter of the QUERY STRING only (FORK F1 — privacy-adjacent, so
     it is yours). Today the site's biggest demand signal is thrown away.
  c. AI search_log rows searched-but-uncited + site zero-results →
     gaps.py → asked_new.py drafts, weekly, before you carry them from the
     groups by hand. The 4-a-week hand-fed loop becomes the exception.
  d. The AI reads provider names out of directory pages it cites (2.7) and
     hands them to the harvester as candidates with the page as evidence.
  Moves: Strain D closed; the miss becomes the work queue.

PHASE 6 — GEOGRAPHY FROM A THAI SOURCE                 (FORK F2 · 1 day once chosen)

================================================================================
5. THE FORKS — YOURS, NOT MINE
================================================================================

  F1  Zero-result query counter on the site search. Stores the query string
      and a count, nothing else — no IP, no session, no time finer than a
      day. Still a first for a site that "does not measure the reader".
      Options: (a) do it, stated on /privacy · (b) do it only for the AI
      widget where a question is already stored · (c) no.
      Cost of (c): the largest demand signal stays invisible.

  F2  A Thai geocoder for 1,207 unpinned records. Longdo (API key, Thai
      company) or data.go.th boundaries (free, static, coarser). Cost of
      neither: 1,207 records never appear in "near me".

  F3  Wongnai / GoWabi as sources. Both hold the Thai-named trade with menus.
      Both have terms of service to read first. Cost of no: thdata + สบส. +
      Facebook carry most of it anyway; slower.

  F4  The evidence bar for `offers`. Proposed: own words only (name, site,
      page, menu photo, licence). Reader review → attrs. Directory sidebar →
      nothing. Lower the bar and the massage rule breaks; keep it and some
      shops stay unlabelled until their own page says it.

  F5  Order inside Phase 2: massage-first (type case, harvest half done) or
      breadth-first (all 18 silent shelves one pass each). I recommend
      massage-first: it proves the register pattern on the hardest shelf.

  F6  Approved AI answers → indexable ถามมด pages (already on your open-fork
      list). Turns the AI's best answers into corpus. Needs your eye per entry.

  Expiry: none of these blocks Phase 0 or Phase 1. F5 blocks Phase 2's
  first day. F1 blocks Phase 5b only. F2 blocks Phase 6 only.


================================================================================
6. WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO
================================================================================

  Does not infer a modality from a shop's hours, street or staff (the
  massage rule). Does not scrape Google or Facebook LISTINGS
  (ToS, your exposure) — a shop's OWN page, read for its own words, is a
  different thing and is where Phase 2 gets offers. Does not add ratings,
  ranking, engagement, or a reader profile. Does not promise a tooltip.
  Does not make the AI the plan: the AI is the third door on one index, and
  it is only as good as Phase 2.
