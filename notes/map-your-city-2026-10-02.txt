MAP YOUR CITY, MOT DANG EDITION
Grab's program as Nan screenshotted it on 1 Oct 2026, 20:33–20:35 (12 screens, posted to the ops
channel 21:20–21:24), and what Mot Dang would need to build its own version.

WHAT GRAB HAS (read off the screens; quoted text is theirs)

  Hub "Map Your City"
    Your performance: Contributions ("Make an impact"), Leaderboard ("Get recognised")
    "Get rewarded as you contribute", three programs:
      Add Places      "Add a missing place to GrabMaps."
      Mini Tasks      NEW. "Complete tasks (e.g. verify places) on the map."
      Update Places   "Provide the latest info about a place."

  Add Places
    Two steps: drop the pin, fill in the details, submit. "Thorough validation."
    Campaign 01 Apr – 31 Dec. "There are currently no rewards available for this campaign."
    Rewards go only to a "qualified place" (one they approved). Status shows in Contributions
    after 14–21 days.
    Rules: check the nearby list first, because duplicates earn nothing. The first submitter
    gets the credit. Name in full; a shop in a mall is written "Shop - Mall". Use the official
    address; block, level and postcode are optional.
    A storefront or name-sign photo is required. They reject photos that are dark, blurred,
    show no place information, or were downloaded from the internet.

  Update Places
    Search a nearby place, tap the pencil. Edit the name, address or category, or report it as
    permanently closed, not open to the public, or moved.
    "We are not giving monetary rewards for edits at the moment."

  Mini Tasks
    Task pins on the map. Start one "when it's safe on the road", submit it for review, and
    "wait for the reward, if there's one".
    Some tasks are answered by a person from Grab instead.

  Not in the screenshots: any reward amount, currency, task list or leaderboard contents.

WHAT MOT DANG ALREADY HAS FOR EACH PIECE
  Add Places      /suggest on the claims Worker; the LINE bot takes pins and photos.
  Update Places   claims: closed, moved, phone, hours. Rain and flood taps went live 28 Sep.
  Mini Tasks      Ground Truth, spec'd 31 Aug and never built. Its fuel is measured: on
                  31 Aug, 12,531 CM places had no hours and 3,125 had vague pins.
  Rewards         Bot Bounty, live for bots at your prices: ฿0 / 20 / 50 / 100 / 300.
  Photo rules     EXIF required on Bot Bounty. The photo pass already reads signs.
  Contributions   nothing for people yet; bots have /anthill/bounty/mine.
  Leaderboard     nothing. Ground Truth's spec ranks map cells, not people.

WHAT A BUILD WOULD BE
  One page, motdang.net/map-your-city (Thai first), with Grab's three tiles wired to what
  exists: Add = /suggest with pin + required sign photo; Update = claims on any place page;
  Tasks = pins drawn from the gaps (no hours, vague pin, no photo, never seen by a person),
  nearest first, by road. A "my contributions" list keyed to the LINE sign-in. Duplicate
  check before submit (the search API already answers this). Grab's photo rules copied.
