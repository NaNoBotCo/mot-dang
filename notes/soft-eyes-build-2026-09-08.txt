THE RULES THAT MADE IT HONEST, each written after the first cut got it wrong
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A GAZETTEER, NOT A GUESS. A parsed อำเภอ is written only when it matches one
  of the province's real 25 (CM) or 18 (CR) in admin_areas.json. 97 grabs
  matched nothing and were dropped rather than written; several of those are
  Pai, Ban Thi, Pa Sang — other provinces, correctly refused.

  THE PIN IS A SECOND WITNESS. Of 14,237 parsed amphoe, 13,658 fall inside that
  อำเภอ's own boundary radius. 363 do not, and those carry attrs.amphoeDisputed:
  either the pin is wrong or the address is, and the pair is worth more to
  whoever fixes it than either half alone.

  WHAT WAS SAID vs WHAT IS. The gazetteer holds 15 of Chiang Mai's tambon and 1
  of Chiang Rai's, so a ตำบล cannot be validated the way an อำเภอ can. A reading
  that matches a tambon the corpus already holds as a fact goes to attrs.tambon;
  everything else goes to attrs.tambonSaid. No reader is misled and no work is
  lost — promoting them is a one-line pass the day that gazetteer is filled.

  A CHAIN NEEDS THREE SEPARATE PINS. Three rows at one coordinate are a
  duplicate, a food court, or one shop entered three times. Never a chain.

  A NAME THIS REPO WROTE IS NOT A BRAND. The first cut branded 87 car parks as a
  chain of 87 branches: the WO-67 parking locators write "ที่จอดรถ · ย่าน<area>"
  and stamp it `derived`. The provenance table already knew; now the pass asks.
  Likewise a trade word — ซักผ้าหยอดเหรียญ stands at 50 pins and is fifty coin
  laundries, not fifty branches. trade_lexicon.json is the list of such words,
  so the test is a lookup, not a judgement.

  A HOST WORD MUST BE A WORD. Thai is written without spaces, so a substring is
  not a word. The first cut made วัดสันห้าง — a temple whose name merely CONTAINS
  ห้าง — the landlord of 43 shops, and "UP 420 (Weed Station)" the landlord of
  92. Now a Thai host word must OPEN the name and a Latin one must stand whole,
  a pin holding more than 20 records is a geocoder's shrug rather than a
  building, and every member must have an exact pin. 926 candidate pins became
  307 tenancies under 33 hosts, all of them markets, hospitals and one
  university faculty. The refusals are counted, not silent.

  NOTHING DERIVED IS EVER GIVEABLE. Every write is stamped how="derived", and
  provenance.giveable() requires how="stated". A reading of ours can never be
  offered to OpenStreetMap as somebody's survey. There is a test.

  FIRST WRITER WINS. No pass overwrites a value already present — a register's
  fact, a curated correction and an owner's claim all outrank a derivation, and
  derive runs last in import_all for that reason.

  IT HOLDS THE BUILD LOCK. This rewrites what build.py reads. Checking for a
  running build was not enough — a build can start in the gap — so cache/build.lock
  is taken for the duration in build.py's own format and released in a finally.
  The first run of this was held behind a live build for exactly that reason.

A LIVE SEARCH BUG, FOUND ON THE WAY, FIXED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Chasing why the lexicon mapped คาเฟ่ (café) to the CANNABIS shelf led to
data/search_shelves.json, which search.html fetches on every search. Deployed
and confirmed on motdang.net the same morning:

    คาเฟ่   -> ["cannabis"]                  a café search went to cannabis, only
    ฟาร์ม   -> ["cannabis"]                  so did a farm search
    คลินิก  -> ["cannabis","clinic","medical"]  a CLINIC search led with cannabis
    caf, farms -> ["cannabis"]

Not a typo. The cannabis shelf's Thai teaser is a correct list of the kinds of
premises cannabis is sold from — "ร้านกัญชา คาเฟ่ ร้านน้ำใบกระท่อม คลินิก ฟาร์ม" —
and search-core/mine.py reads a teaser as shelf membership. GENERIC_PREMISES was
written in August for exactly this, after `clinic` did it in English; the Thai
half never got the same treatment, and `gate` (WO-68) was the third instance of
one bug. This is the fourth.

Two rules, in ../search-core/mine.py:

  GENERIC_PREMISES_TH — a premise word is what a business is HOUSED IN, never
  what it IS, so it cannot narrow a search on its own. NOT หมู่บ้าน: it looks
  like one and is not, because a หมู่บ้าน IS the housing development, and
  dropping it cost the real-estate shelf its best Thai term for one run.

  A SHELF'S OWN NAME OUTRANKS AN UNRELATED SHELF'S TEASER. There is a shelf
  literally called คลินิก. A name is a stronger claim than a teaser. Restricted
  to UNRELATED shelves after the first cut stripped `medical` off โรงพยาบาล and
  `massage` off ตอกเส้น — a parent and its own child are not rivals, and that
  version would have quietly undone the tok-sen work.

The whole diff over 656 terms: FOUR wrong mappings gone (คาเฟ่, ฟาร์ม, caf,
farms), คลินิก narrowed from three shelves to the one it is named after,
โรงพยาบาล narrowed to the hospital shelf, four terms gained. Nothing else moved.
