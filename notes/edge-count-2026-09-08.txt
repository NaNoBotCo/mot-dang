WHY IT WAS WORTH BUILDING TODAY
  motdang.net took 626,640 requests in the 28 days to 2026-09-08 and there was
  no way to say how many were people. Worker analytics carries no User-Agent;
  zone analytics does (top paths, referrers, countries, bots) and the fleet
  token is refused `zone.analytics.read` on that zone, while wrangler's OAuth
  expired 2026-08-23 and will not refresh outside an interactive login.

  The shape of the traffic was readable even so, and it does not look like
  people. Baseline 10-20k/day. On 09-06 02:00-06:00 UTC a flat plateau of
  ~14,500/hr for three hours, ~48k in five — flat, no diurnal curve. From 09-06
  15:00 the floor moved from ~400/hr to 1,000-3,000/hr and stayed there through
  09-07 and 09-08. Aug 12 (75,740), Aug 20 and Aug 28 (43k each) have the same
  plateau shape. Something sweeps the site periodically and something new has
  been coming steadily since the 6th. Which crawlers those are is exactly the
  question a User-Agent answers and nothing on the account was recording one.

WHAT WAS ALREADY THERE — traffic-eye
  ../traffic-eye is a fleet collector Worker built 2026-07-30 that records these
  nine categories from the User-Agent, with a dashboard over them. It was
  deployed and never routed at anything: every route in wrangler.collector.toml
  is commented out. It could NOT have been routed at motdang.net in any case — a
  Cloudflare hostname belongs to exactly one Worker and motdang.net/* belongs to
  mot-dang-site. So nothing new was designed here; the classifier was vendored
  and the write moved inside the site Worker.

ONE DATASET, TWO WRITERS
  The rows go into traffic-eye's `traffic_eye` dataset in ITS blob order, so its
  dashboard reads motdang.net beside the other sites. Position IS the schema
  there: a row written in a different order is not an error, it is a wrong
  number that looks right. test_edge_count.mjs parses the blob comments out of
  both files and fails when they drift.

THE ABSENCES, WHICH ARE THE POINT
  No IP, no cookie, no identifier — two requests by one reader are two rows that
  cannot be joined, and there is a test that sends a cookie, an IP header and an
  Authorization header and asserts none of them appear. The referrer is cut to
  its host (a search engine's full referrer carries the words somebody typed)
  and the query string is dropped from the path for the same reason.

  Categories come from the User-Agent, which is self-declared. Anything can call
  itself Googlebot. "human" means a browser's User-Agent with no bot marker —
  the ceiling of what a count with no cookie can know, and the reader says so.

IT CANNOT BREAK THE SITE
  serve() runs first and its response is returned whatever happens next. The
  write is inside waitUntil inside a try/catch whose failure path is to do
  nothing. Unbound binding, Analytics Engine outage, unfamiliar User-Agent — the
  reader still gets the page, and there are tests for each.
