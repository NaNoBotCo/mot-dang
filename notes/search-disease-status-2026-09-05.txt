THE HORIZON — WHAT CURING A CITY GROWS INTO
================================================================================

  1. COVERAGE AS A PUBLIC NUMBER. /coverage.html per city: for every home-ground
     term, held vs the outside count, dated, unflattering on purpose. That page IS
     the BHAG's unit of measurement: a search-disease index a city can be scored
     on, and a second city compared against. The demonstration is one table:
     the same twenty questions, Mot Dang's answer beside the incumbent's, by hand.

  2. A REGISTER OF WHAT THE CITY DOES, NOT WHAT IT IS CALLED. offers[] with a
     source on every item turns 24,000 names into a menu of the city. We already
     hold 1,062 links to shops' own Facebook pages, the richest own-words source
     there is. The claims door (go/listings) and the LINE bot let the shop write
     its own offers: the supply side of the cure, and the one Google structurally
     cannot copy, because ours downloads.

  3. THE DEMAND CORPUS AS THE EXAM. 367 of your own questions with dates on them,
     26 asked.json entries, the AI's search log, and (F1) the site's own misses.
     A city is cured when the exam passes, not when a shelf count grows. Every
     miss is a work item within a day; the 4-a-week hand-carried loop becomes the
     exception.

  4. REGISTERS FIRST, EVERYWHERE. The finding under every number today: a
     local-native register beats a global map every time (ONAB filled monastic
     schools; data.go.th made CR massage 100% Thai-named). The kit's day-one
     question for any city is "name three registers written by insiders."

  5. THE KIT (CITY.md). vocabulary.json · three registers · twenty questions ·
     the discipline rules. Chiang Rai proves it with no new code. Then the
     northern circuit where the inverse-coverage law bites hardest and no
     directory exists at all: Lamphun, Lampang, Pai and Mae Hong Son, Nan, Phrae.
     A city becomes a folder of JSON and a crawl list, not a codebase.

  6. THE AI AS A CRAWLER WITH A PROVENANCE RULE. It already cites directory pages
     holding twenty names and returns one. Reading names out of pages it cites,
     handing them to the harvester as candidates with the page as evidence,
     makes the third door feed the corpus instead of only reading it.

  7. THE LEXICON MADE PUBLIC. WO-47's thesaurus page plus vocabulary.json is an
     open home-ground lexicon anyone can download: the words a city uses for
     what it does, in both scripts, with counts. /why.html made concrete.

  8. THE LAW, WRITTEN UP. Sixteen OSM elements with นวด in the name across the
     province of tok sen's birth, against 337 in a Thai directory, is a measured
     instance of the inverse-coverage law. It is a chapter, a pitch and a paper.
     Whether and where it is written is yours.

  None of this changes the discipline: no inference about what a shop "really"
  does, and a source on every fact.
