SEARCH IS BROKEN IN CHIANG MAI — MEASURED, NOT ASSERTED
2026-09-18

Method, so anyone can run it again:
  38 queries, four strata, put to a general web search engine (Brave Search
  API, the same index the white-label assistant uses) on 18 September 2026.
  Top ten results captured per query; the top five fetched with an ordinary
  browser user-agent and their HTTP status recorded. The same questions put to
  motdang.net/api/v1/places.
  Probe: notes/search-broken-cm-probe.py · raw: notes/search-broken-cm-2026-09-18.json

  The four strata:
    A  Thai home-ground trade terms — things Chiang Mai is the world capital of
    B  ordinary needs in English — drawn from questions the demand corpus named
    C  answer-shaped questions — what + where + when together
    D  Chiang Rai — the thin-city case


================================================================================
1. WHAT COMES BACK
================================================================================

  380 results across 38 questions.

      38%   a page on somebody's site
      16%   a Facebook page
      15%   a listicle or a city blog
      13%   a business's own site
       7%   a booking aggregator — TripAdvisor, Viator, Trip.com, Klook
       6%   a forum thread — Reddit, ASEAN Now
       4%   a TikTok or YouTube video
       1%   a structured directory
       1%   a marketplace listing — a shop selling the tools of the trade

  The first result was a listicle, a forum, or a blog for 26% of the questions.

  QUESTIONS WHERE THE TOP FIVE HELD A WORKING LINK TO A SPECIFIC BUSINESS:
  20 of 38.

  Not a scandal on its own. The shape of the other 18 is the finding.


================================================================================
2. THE LINKS DO NOT RESOLVE
================================================================================

  190 top-five links fetched. 137 answered 200. The rest:

      21   HTTP 400   every one of them facebook.com
      15   HTTP 403   the page refuses an ordinary automated client
       7   no answer  DNS failure or connection refused
       6   HTTP 404   gone
       2   HTTP 432 · 1 HTTP 410 · 1 HTTP 521

  Two separate facts live in that column, and they should not be blurred:

  SIMPLY GONE — 404, 410, or nothing at the address: 14 of 168 non-Facebook
  links, 8%. One in twelve of the links a search engine offers for Chiang Mai
  leads nowhere at all.

  CLOSED TO MACHINES — 400, 403, 432: 18 more. Including every Facebook link
  but one.


================================================================================
3. THE PART THAT MATTERS: THE CITY'S BUSINESSES LIVE ON FACEBOOK
================================================================================

  Facebook was the single most common host in the results — 60 of 380, ahead of
  cmhy.city (15), TripAdvisor (14), TikTok (11), Reddit (10).

  21 of the 22 Facebook links fetched answered HTTP 400 to a client that was
  not logged in.

  So for a whole class of Chiang Mai question the state of the art is exactly this: the answer exists, it is a Facebook page, and neither a
  logged-out person nor any machine can read it.

  Mot Dang's own corpus says the same thing from the other side. Counted from
  data/canonical on 18 September 2026, 88,246 records:

      Facebook page ......... 69,698   79.0%
      phone ................. 61,114   69.3%
      Thai in the name ...... 61,839   70.1%
      address ............... 49,122   55.7%
      email ................. 25,378   28.8%
      website ............... 15,564   17.6%
      hours .................. 4,407    5.0%
      a pin ................. 87,674   99.4%

  Four in five of the businesses in this city have a Facebook page and fewer
  than one in five has a website. That is not a preference anyone chose. It is
  where the commercial web of northern Thailand actually lives, and it is
  behind a login.


================================================================================
4. THE INVERSE-COVERAGE LAW, VISIBLE IN THE RESULTS
================================================================================

  Stratum A was the Thai trade terms — ตอกเส้น, ย่ำขาง, จับเส้น, ประคบสมุนไพร,
  the knife smiths, the watch menders, the hand-loom cotton.

  Chiang Mai is the world capital of every one of them. The search results for
  ตอกเส้น เชียงใหม่, in order:

      a Facebook page                             HTTP 400
      a TripAdvisor review page                   HTTP 403
      a university course application, 2019       HTTP 404
      thdata.co, a Thai directory listing         200
      Shopee, selling the wooden hammer           200

  Two of the five are about a shop you could visit. One opens.

  Then the same question with the constraint a person actually has —
  "tok sen massage old city chiang mai open after 9pm" — returns five results,
  every one of them a tour-booking page, and not one of them carries opening
  hours at all.

  This is the law from the 5 September diagnosis operating in public: at the
  epicentre of a practice, nobody writes it into a category, because everybody
  already knows. The categories were written elsewhere.


================================================================================
5. THE SAME QUESTIONS, PUT TO MOT DANG
================================================================================

  Live, through motdang.net/api/v1/places, 18 September 2026:

      motorcycle parking near Tha Phae Gate
          44 records, each with its distance from the gate — 80 m, 100 m, 150 m
          Search returned: a scooter rental company, a hotel page, a travel
          blog, and a parking page for the MotoGP circuit in Buriram.

      tok sen, old city, still open at 21:30
          1 — นุ่น นวดตอกเส้น. The same shop TripAdvisor was ranking, except
          the record carries the hours the question turned on, and it opens.

      coin-operated drinking water within 1.5 km of the moat
          35, most of them 24/7. No other index holds this category at all.

      dentist in Chiang Rai ...................... 0
      public toilet near Chiang Mai Gate ......... 0

  The last two are in this note on purpose. The corpus holds 433 health records
  in Chiang Rai and 2,967 toilets; the query missed them because the search box
  matches on a name and neither is NAMED that. Mot Dang carries the same
  name-strain it diagnoses. What it has, and nobody else does, is the record
  underneath — and a per-field receipt saying where each fact came from.


================================================================================
6. WHAT THIS SUPPORTS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
================================================================================

  Supported by the measurement above:
    · a search engine answers about half of these questions with a working
      link to a business, and answers the constrained ones worse
    · one in twelve of the links it offers is gone
    · the most common destination for a Chiang Mai business question is a
      Facebook page that answers 400 to anyone not logged in
    · four in five businesses in the corpus are reachable that way and no other
    · Mot Dang answers geographic and hours-constrained questions that general
      search does not, where the field has been collected

  NOT supported, and not to be claimed:
    · that Mot Dang answers most questions today — it does not; the same
      indexing disease runs in its own search box
    · that anybody has paid for any of this
    · that the traffic to motdang.net is people rather than crawlers; that is
      uninstrumented (see go/motdang-product)

  38 queries is a probe, not a survey. It is reproducible, the raw results are
  beside this file, and the code that ran it is one file.
