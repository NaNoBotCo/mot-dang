THE FOUR THINGS THAT CHANGED

1  THE SUB-SHELF IS A FIELD.  87,319 records stand on a sub-shelf and the tag
   layer read none of them, so a massage shop with a technique (tok-sen), a
   hotel with a class and a food stall with a cuisine all had nothing to be
   found by across shelves. Now the sub is mined like any other field, and it
   JOINS the tag that already says the same thing rather than minting a twin.
   This is the single biggest lift: it is most of the 18,427.

2  THE NAME IS A FIELD TOO — the inference you asked for.  In this catalogue a
   name is usually the shop's own description of itself: ซักผ้าหยอดเหรียญ is a
   coin laundry, ก๋วยเตี๋ยวเรือป้านิด is a noodle shop, Baan Suan Guesthouse is
   a guesthouse. 25,948 tags are now read out of names, and the tag says which
   word it read. Two guards keep it inference and not invention:
     · the shelf must already say it — a tag can only be read off a name on a
       shelf where the FIELD-earned members of that same tag already stand, so
       "Thai" is read off a food place and never off นวดแผนไทย;
     · a word has to be a word — Thai matches are at least three characters,
       Latin ones are whole words of at least four, so "bar" never lights up
       Barber and "spa" never lights up Spanish.
   Spot-checked: แอร์ found 182 places, every one an air-con repair shop.

3  ONE AXIS, NOT TWO HALVES.  The 46 *_restaurant words in the Overture fold
   were being minted as separate KIND tags standing beside the hand-written
   cuisine list that already had the word — japanese_restaurant beside
   japanese. They are cuisines now, and they join the tag that exists.
   Same for the facility and specialty families. `restaurant` itself is
   un-skipped: 3,873 records carry it and no sub-shelf, so it was the only
   word they had.

4  THE SITE'S OWN WORDS.  A mined tag was supposed to take its label from the
   shelf labels before falling back to the raw value, and nothing had ever
   handed them over — so 180 sub tags arrived as "Hotel full" and "Bakery
   dessert" with no Thai at all. They now read กาแฟ-คาเฟ่ · Coffee & Cafés,
   ซ่อมรถ-อู่ · Auto & Motorbike, ทนาย-บัญชี · Lawyers & Accountants.


TWO BUGS FOUND WHILE DOING IT

  · A brand tag called "Restaurant".  Six records have the word Restaurant
    typed into their brand box; the first cut of the merging handed 7,516
    restaurants to that tag, which says they are all branches of one chain.
    A value may now only join a tag in the SAME family, and a brand can no
    longer take the name of a kind of place (a `brand_skip` list, plus every
    hand-written tag slug). Brand tags: 194 real chains.
  · A rule that mined nothing.  The `street` family read attrs.street, which
    no record in this catalogue carries — 14,358 carry attrs.road. It had been
    mining nothing at all.


THE MERGE AND THE LABELS — both done, same evening

  · THE COFFEE MERGE.  Four fields were saying one thing in four wordings:
    กาแฟ-คาเฟ่ (8,726), คาเฟ่ (4,518), ร้านกาแฟ (3,752) and Coffee (3,043).
    They are one tag now — กาแฟ-คาเฟ่ · Coffee & Cafés, 8,728 places — and it
    carries every (field, value) pair it swallowed, so a member can still be
    shown the word it actually holds: cafe, coffee_shop, coffee or tea_room.
    Deciding that "Coffee shop" and "Café" are the same tag is a person's
    judgment and not a rule, so the list is written down in data/tags.json
    under `merge` rather than derived. Six others went with it: barber →
    barbers, bubble tea, hotel → hotels, guest house → guesthouses, and the
    Overture vegetarian and halal into the diet tags that already existed.

  · THE 406 ARE LABELLED.  Every mined tag now has both names. 691 carry a
    written label; the other 947 are place names and Thai trade words whose
    English half is an RTGS reading, which is the designed fallback and is
    marked as a reading rather than dressed as a name.
    Two things fixed while doing it: a postcode chip read "50300" and nothing
    else (it reads รหัสไปรษณีย์ 50300 · 50300 postcode now), and the
    อ./ต./ถ. decoration was growing into the ADDRESS — the tag is still at
    /tags/in-san-sai/ and only its label says (district).
    Two values stopped being tags at all: `unknown` from the wat register,
    which filed 247 wats under a fact nobody recorded, and a trade value of
    "50".


STILL OPEN

    longer fails on it, so the build will print the real number. Your
