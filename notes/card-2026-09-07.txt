A RESULT IS A PLACE, NOT A LINK
WO-69 · WO-70 · WO-71 · WO-72 · WO-73 — built 2026-09-07, NOT deployed
Written for Nan. Michael's brief, this afternoon, is the whole of the spec:

  "the best and highest use of Motdang is finding things in Chiang Mai...
   if I see something I like on the list of results, I want to have map
   controls of it accessible, links to related (nearby/near time) things,
   interactivity, and relationships. All items listed by motdang should
   contain relational tags for discovery, and discovery should EXPLICITLY
   allow searching/finding by tags. Site still contains too many words. If
   you need to explain something using words, you're fucking up."

Everything below is measured on a scratch build, not asserted. Nothing is
deployed; docs/ was never touched.

==============================================================================
0. THE SHAPE OF IT
==============================================================================

The site already held almost everything he asked for and showed none of it.

  · 127 tag slugs on 7,873 records, 193 tag pages, 1,724 trade tags — and
    the search box could not filter by any of them. A tag word was folded
    into one keyword string at 0.45 weight and matched as text.
  · 50,946 measured "near" edges in the graph, rendered nowhere.
  · /api/v1/places has taken tag=, sub=, near= and radius= since August. The
    site's own JavaScript has never called it.
  · bearings_layer.py — the module whose docstring calls orientation "this
    site's actual secret sauce", which you said YES to on 9/6 — was written,
    tested, and never wired into build.py. Nor was here_layer.py.

So this pass is mostly plumbing what was already paid for, and cutting words.

==============================================================================
1. WHAT A RESULT LOOKS LIKE NOW
==============================================================================

BEFORE, every row on every search, all of it:

    <li><a>กีตาร์เฮาส์</a> <span>Guitar House</span></li>

AFTER:

    กีตาร์เฮาส์  Guitar House   · 80 ม.  ●
    [ เครื่องดนตรี ]  [ ถ.สุเทพ ]  [ 🎸 ร้านเครื่องดนตรี ]              📍

  · the metres whenever a point is known — a landmark you named, a near=,
    or your own position;
  · a lamp: lit = open now, dim = closed now, ABSENT = nobody has recorded
    hours. Never "closed" from an absence;
  · up to three chips, and every chip is a filter you can tap: the
    sub-shelf, then the street or the district or the nearest landmark
    within 1.5 km, then the best tag it carries;
  · 📍 opens the map on it.

Touch the card body and it opens in place, without leaving the page:

  · a map centred on it (one MapLibre instance for the whole list, moved
    from card to card — not one per row);
  · the three nearest OTHER places we hold, with metres;
  · "same kind nearby" — every place on its shelf, nearest first;
  · what is on here, if anything is;
  · add to my plan, and the page link.

DISCOVERY BY TAG, EXPLICITLY, as he asked:

    search.html?tag=vegan            no words at all — the filter IS the query
    search.html?tag=vegan&sub=cafe   102 vegan places · 21 vegan cafés
    #vegan in the box                lifted out of the words into a filter
    "vegan cafe"                     the constraint you stated becomes a filter
    ?st=…  ?ar=…  ?cat=…  ?near=…    street, district, shelf, a point

  Every tag pill on a place page grew a 🔍 that opens exactly that search,
  and the trade tags ("ที่ร้านลงไว้ว่าทำ") now link to the tag filter
  instead of dropping their words into the free-text box.

  A filter is a chip; tapping it removes it. A tag nobody has answers
  ZERO — not the whole catalogue, which is what a silent filter does.

4. INTENT BEFORE WORDS — the rare word IS the question (WO-70)
==============================================================================

Michael, 9/6: "best place to buy a Martin guitar?" put Guitar House about
230th, behind two hundred cafés. `buy` matched 261 rows, `martin` 4,
`guitar` 4 — and the score could not tell them apart, because nothing in it
knew that a word matching four rows says more than a word matching 261.

When no row matches every word, the pile is now ranked by how RARE the words
each row matched are. Measured on the scratch build:

    "best place to buy a Martin guitar"
      top three: Guitar House · Intune Guitar Shop · Cin Guitars
    "cheap tok sen massage old city"
      top three: Phailin Tok Sen · Green Bamboo · one more tok sen shop

Rows that matched every word are untouched, and every existing search case
still passes.

6. GEOCODING (WO-73) — it was confidently wrong, and now it says so
==============================================================================

The gazetteer's docstring claimed it covers the truth 80% of the time. I
measured it: 63%. Every line of that docstring was stale in the optimistic
direction. The worst five results of every run were ONE failure repeated:

    ถนนโชตนา in อ.ฝาง, matched to the Chiang Mai stub of a road that runs
    128 km north, and declared ±280 m — because the uncertainty was half the
    length of the fragment WE HOLD, not of the road.

Three fixes, all of them about witnesses:

  · THE DISTRICT IS A WITNESS. Almost every Thai address names its อำเภอ,
    and nothing here read it. A road, a tambon or a postcode that falls
    outside the named district is the wrong one and is refused. (43 อำเภอ
    centres and radii were sitting in data/curated/admin_areas.json, fetched
    in August, read by nothing.)
  · A STREET NOBODY VOUCHES FOR is not a ±280 m answer: with no
    corroborating tambon or postcode it answers at ±3,000 m, above the
    publish cap, so it is not published rather than published wrong.
  · THE SIGN'S SPELLING IS A RULE, NOT A LIST. The h after t/p/k and the
    space between syllables are whoever painted the sign's choice, so both
    are folded before matching — Tha Phae / Thapae / Tapae, Huay Kaew /
    Huai Kaeo, Wua Lai / Wualai all reach their road. The residue the rules
    cannot reach is 25 hand-written rows in data/curated/street_aliases.json
    (Nimman, Kaeo Nawarat, Charoen Prathet…), which was named as the fix on
    9/5 and never built.

    calibration   63% → 75%     street median 247 m → 219 m
    unplaceable   971 → 189     (the district now places what nothing else could)

  tests/test_geocode_local.py is new and holds the ถนนโชตนา case.

  A DRY RUN says 194 new pins are available under the ±3 km cap. I have NOT
  written them: geocode_gaps writes data/curated/pins.json, import_all
  rewrites data/canonical/, and that is a corpus change while a build was
  running. Your call — see the forks.
