────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
0. WHY THESE FOUR ARE ONE PROBLEM

  You picked four things that look unrelated and are not. They share a property
  that neither a shop nor an institution has:

      NOBODY WILL EVER CLAIM THEM.

  No proprietor fills in a bus shelter's opening hours. There is no owner of a
  tree. A soi has no proprietor and a well has no counter. So the entire
  enrichment model this directory was built on — owner-confirmed, a phone to
  ring, a listing somebody wants to claim — is VOID for all four, and the only
  sources left are the map, the survey (a person stood there and wrote it down),
  the municipal asset register, and what the neighbourhood knows.

  Call that posture THE COMMONS. It is a third thing beside commerce and the
  institutions, and it needs its own everything.

  Two consequences follow, and both are structural:

  · THEY ARE NOT DISCRETE. A soi is a LINE. A well serves a neighbourhood. A
    shrine may be one of a cluster under one tree. The record shape — one name,
    one pin, one address — was built for a shopfront and does not fit any of
    them. This is why `geoPrecision: "line"` had to exist before a soi could be
    a record at all.

  · THEY ARE NAVIGATED BY, NOT TO. Nobody searches for a bus shelter by name. A
    shelter is the answer INSIDE another answer — "how do I get to X". That makes
    bearings (WO-64) worth more to this class than pins are, and it means the
    reader face for them is a different face.

  And one more, which is the reason to hurry: THEY ARE THE MOST FRAGILE THING IN
  THE CATALOGUE. A shop that closes gets re-mapped by the next crawler. A yang-na
  avenue felled for a road widening, a well capped, a หอเสื้อบ้าน cleared for a
  car park — nobody re-finds those. The commons is the part of a city directory
  that is also a record of the city.

1. THE BIG FINDING: THE SOIS WERE ALREADY HERE

  I went looking for named ways expecting to have to crawl for them.

      data/streets.json holds 1,077 NAMED WAYS across both provinces.
      432 of them are SOIS.
      Every single one is Thai-named.
      534 carry geometry · 508 know what they cross · 355 know their parent road.
      6,227 PLACES ARE ALREADY PLACED ALONG THEM — 685 by the place's own OSM
      address (`stated`), 5,542 by sitting within 30 m and nearer than any
      other named way (`nearest`, with the metres recorded).
      Median length 268 m. Busiest: ถนนช้างคลาน with 156 places on it.

  None of it is a record. The ways exist only as a JOIN TABLE serving other
  pages — no id, no kind, no page, not in the search index. A reader who types
  ซอยวัดอุโมงค์ gets nothing, and the catalogue has known the answer since WO-6.

  This is the empty-shelves Cause 2 in its largest form yet: not a shelf that
  never filled, but AN ENTIRE CLASS FULLY MODELLED ON DISK AND NEVER ADMITTED.
  1,077 records, zero fetching, zero inference.

2. WHAT WAS BUILT TODAY

  TWO NEW KINDS, on the axis from this morning:

    `sacred`  a place of observance that is not a wat and not a body of people —
              a spirit house, a wound tree, a city pillar, a guardian shrine, a
              Chinese shrine. 40 records claimed, checked one by one by eye.
    `way`     a road, soi, alley or bridge — a place that is a LINE, not a point.
              You find things ALONG it and you navigate BY it.

  `edge_layer.py` PROJECTS the ways rather than importing them: streets.json
  stays the truth, the 1,077 records are derived every build, and a curated
  overlay can hang facts a projection can never know — does it flood, can a car
  get down it, who the donor was — off the same id. Reversible by deletion.

  THE FALSE POSITIVES, because this is where a name rule earns its keep. The
  first pass on `sacred` claimed 57 records. Seventeen were wrong and every one
  is now held out by a written rule:
      ธนาคาร            contains นาค — 219 banks matched a naga probe
      ศาลจังหวัด        is a COURTHOUSE, not a shrine
      โรงเรียนเจ้าพ่อหลวงอุปถัมภ์  is a school under royal patronage
      อารักษ์           is a road AND a vet clinic AND an alterations shop
      Bodhi Tree Café · ใบโพธิ์นวดแผนโบราณ · Slee Banyan — a café, a massage
                        shop and a restaurant trading on a sacred tree
      ขาหมูแสนคำ (หน้าศาลเจ้าแม่จัน) — a pork-leg stall giving its landmark
  "Slee Banyan" could not be held out by any word in its name, so the compiler
  learned `cat_not`: a `food` record is not a shrine however it is called.

  KNOWN LIMITATION, written down rather than hidden: `claimed` (owner-confirmed)
  is one of the five universal ants and a commons record can almost never earn
  it, so these kinds realistically cap at 8 of 9. It is left universal because
  moving it would reorder the strip on every shop page, and the shopping axis has
  not been moved. The honest fix, when you want it, is a
  `vouched` path — a keeper recorded (the wat that tends the shrine, the tambon
  that keeps the well, the family on the donor plaque) earning the same ant.

3. THE FOUR, ONE BY ONE — AND THE VERNACULAR, WHICH IS THE WHOLE GAME

  The search-disease lesson applies hardest here: for all four classes, THE THAI
  IS THE ONLY PLACE THEY LIVE. A well is not "a well" to anybody who uses it.
  So each class carries its terms, each with a reading and a gloss, in
  data/curated/kinds.json under `edge`, and the test refuses a term without all
  three or a Thai word written only in Latin.
