THE INSTITUTIONS PROBLEM — what EFEO is an instance of
Mot Dang · problem statement only · 2026-09-06 · no build
Counts below are from data/canonical/cm.json + cr.json as of this morning (24,059 records).
"Corpus holds 0" means not on this disk. It never means the city has none.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
0. ONE LINE

  Mot Dang admits a place to the corpus by its COUNTER — the point where it
  sells something to someone. A place with no counter cannot get in, has no
  shelf to sit on, has no field that could say what it is for, and is fed by
  a genre of source that has never been registered. EFEO fails on all four
  at once. So does every library, archive, institute, consulate, foundation,
  association, congregation, society, club and civic office in the city.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. THE ENTRY TICKETS — how a place gets in today

  Every record arrived through one of three doors:

    (a) A MAPPER WALKED PAST IT and it had a signboard worth tagging.
        OSM — 15,262 records (63%).
    (b) A LICENCE OR REGISTER NAMES IT AS A BUSINESS/SCHOOL/WAT.
        ONAB wats, OBEC/OPEC schools, MOPH clinics, condo register, weedth,
        DBD juristic persons — register 3,969 + opendata 2,182.
    (c) IT MARKETS ITSELF TO CONSUMERS.
        CMHY trade directory 3,293; Facebook business pages; curated 680.

  source_register.json holds 18 sources. All 18 are one of (a)(b)(c):
  business pages, licensed clinics, tour-operator licences, seller
  storefronts, YellowPages, market-stall lists. Not one is a ROSTER OF
  INSTITUTIONS. The class was not under-crawled; it was never in scope.

  The test for membership in the class: fails (a) AND (b) AND (c).
    EFEO: no signboard trade → not tagged. Not a business, school or wat →
    on no register we hold. Serves scholars by appointment, not consumers →
    no marketing surface. Invisible by construction, not by accident.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    class                         held   where they were filed
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    archive จดหมายเหตุ               0   —   (National Archives has a CM branch)
    mosque มัสยิด                    0   —   (CM has well over a dozen)
    consulate กงสุล                  2   1 = US Consulate (via 'web'); 1 = "Chicken Embassy", food
    institute สถาบัน                 8   7 filed school
    church คริสตจักร                 8   5 are CEMETERIES; 1 filed `wat`; 1 filed `sights/historic`
    club ชมรม/สโมสร                  8
    research ศูนย์วิจัย              8   4 filed essentials
    association สมาคม               11
    non-formal ed กศน.              11
    foundation มูลนิธิ              19   (province registers hundreds)
    library ห้องสมุด                22   17 filed `sights`; 14 are CMU faculty rooms
    ho trai หอไตร                    1
    cemetery สุสาน/ป่าช้า           303   ← the tell

  The cemetery row is the proof. 303 cemeteries, 0 archives. A cemetery is a
  LANDUSE POLYGON — a mapper draws it. An archive is a FUNCTION — nobody draws
  it. The corpus holds what has a footprint, not what has a purpose.

  The `community` cat (375 records) is where institutions go to die:
  278 of 375 (74%) are cremation grounds. Subs: cremation 278, centre 59,
  clubs 16, volunteer 14, arts 3. There is no sub for church, mosque,
  foundation, association, consulate, library, archive or institute. A
  church that is not a cemetery had to be filed as a wat or a historic sight.

  The one consulate that made it came through source type `web` — the only
  door in the register that is not a map, a licence or a storefront. That
  door has 216 records. It is the door this class needs, and it is 0.9% of
  the corpus.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. WHY THIS IS NOT THE TOK-SEN DISEASE

  Tok sen (go/disease) was a MISSING ATTRIBUTE ON A PRESENT RECORD: the shop
  was in the corpus as a massage shop; what it offered was not. One miss.

  EFEO is FOUR MISSES STACKED, each of which alone would hide it:

    1. No RECORD    — no source we hold names it.
    2. No SHELF     — 203 shelf keys; none is library/archive/institute/
                      consulate/congregation/foundation. `libraries` exists as
                      a shelf key but resolves to a `library` TAG that 0
                      records carry; the 21 library-ish records are a LENS.
    3. No FIELD     — the schema cannot say what an institution is FOR.
    4. No SOURCE GENRE — see §5.

  Fixing any one leaves it invisible. The name-rule that filled physio and
  made-to-order from names already in the corpus (go/shelves) cannot help
  here: the names are not in the corpus.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. WHAT THE SCHEMA CANNOT SAY

  Top-level fields: name, lat/lng, address, phone, website, hours, cat, sub.
  attrs census, top keys: pinVia, amphoe, postcode, facilityType, tambon,
  kind, facets, sector, sect, foundedBE, tradeTags, desc, watCode, watRank,
  cuisine, licenceNo, brand, brandWikidata, operator, students, classrooms.

  Every one of these describes a TRADE, a WAT or a SCHOOL. A reader's
  question to an institution is none of those. It is:

    WHAT DOES IT HOLD OR DO   — "Lanna palm-leaf manuscripts, microfilm,
                                French & Thai scholarship on the region"
    WHO MAY USE IT            — public / members / researchers / by letter
    ON WHAT TERMS             — appointment, membership fee, reading-room
                                only, no lending, ID required
    WHO IS BEHIND IT          — parent body (EFEO Paris; CCT; MFA of X;
                                Rotary District 3360)
    SINCE WHEN / STILL?       — a foundation can be dormant for a decade and
                                still registered
    IN WHAT LANGUAGE          — the librarian's, the catalogue's, the sermon's

  No field carries any of these. `desc` exists on 2,531 records — nearly all
  the ONAB wat register text. There is no `holds`, `access`, `parent`,
  `eligibility`, `terms`. A perfect crawl would yield a pin and a name, which
  for a research library is worse than nothing: it sends a reader to a locked
  door on a Tuesday.

  This is the search disease in its hardest form. Tok sen was a SERVICE you
  buy — an `offers[]` line fixes it. An institution's value is a HOLDING, a
  CAPABILITY or an ELIGIBILITY. Nobody asks "library near me" to find EFEO.
  They ask "who has the Jinakālamālī", "where can I read Tai Khün", "is
  there a Quaker meeting", "which consul handles a Dutch passport". The unit
  of demand is the holding; the unit of the index is the name.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. WHY CRAWLING HARDER CANNOT FIX IT — AND WHAT CAN

  The inverse-coverage law again, inverted once more. Shops are
  under-documented and over-mapped. Institutions are OVER-DOCUMENTED and
  UNMAPPED. EFEO has a century of publications, an annual report, a staff
  page, footnotes in every serious book on Lanna. The information is not
  scarce; it lives in a GENRE OF SOURCE the pipeline has never read:

    ROSTERS   — institutions are legible to EACH OTHER even when illegible
                to maps. Someone maintains a list: the MFA's consular list;
                the provincial Islamic committee's mosque list; the Church
                of Christ in Thailand's district rolls; the Interior
                Ministry's foundation/association registry (มูลนิธิ/สมาคม
                are REGISTERED, like wats); MHESI's research-unit list;
                National Archives' branch list; กศน. centre lists; Rotary
                District 3360; the Informal Northern Thai Group's speaker
                archive; the Foreign Cemetery's own burial roll.
    OWN PAGES — an institution's About page is written by the institution,
                with access rules on it. This is the "own words" bar already
                set for Facebook (go/provenance): first-hand.
    THE LITERATURE — acknowledgements, footnotes, bibliographies, gazettes.
                The only source genre where a manuscript HOLDING is stated.

  The 1930s Polk and Kelly city directories already knew this. Their
  "Churches / Societies / Consuls / Libraries / Public Offices" sections were
  compiled from rosters, separately from the alphabetical trades which were
  walked. Mot Dang has rebuilt the trades half of a city directory and none
  of the miscellany half.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. WHY THE ECONOMICS ARE THE OPPOSITE OF THE SHOPS

  Shops: 20,000 of them, churn yearly, cheap to be wrong about (a closed
  noodle stall costs a reader a walk). Hand-entry cannot scale; crawl must.

  Institutions: hundreds, not tens of thousands. Churn is decades (EFEO,
  the Consulate, the Foreign Cemetery, the mosques have not moved in a
  generation). Expensive to be wrong about (a wasted trip to an
  appointment-only reading room; a consulate that no longer handles your
  passport). Small N × low churn × high error cost = the ONE class where
  hand-curation from rosters is not a stopgap but the correct method, and
  where a DATE on every access rule is mandatory.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. WHY IT IS THE MISSION, NOT A SIDE SHELF

  HERE and ARRIVALS (go/here, go/arrivals) serve the 99% ARRIVING. This
  class serves the person who STAYS — and the scholar, the convert, the
  bereaved, the stateless, the volunteer. It is the difference between a
  tourist listing and a civic record.

  It is also the memory of the city. The shops will be re-mapped by the
  next crawler. The Payap archive's missionary letters, the ho trai's
  bundles, the EFEO reading room will not be re-found by anyone if they go
  unlisted. A directory that holds 303 cemeteries and 0 archives has the
  memory organs backwards.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. WHAT THE ARCHITECTURE MUST ANSWER (not answered here — these are the forks)

  A. WHERE THE CLASS LIVES — a new top-level cat `institutions` with its own
     children (library, archive, institute, consulate, foundation,
     association, congregation:church/mosque/gurdwara/synagogue/meeting,
     club, civic-office), vs a lens laid across the existing cats. The
     `community` cat cannot host it: it is 74% crematoria.

  B. THE FIELDS — `holds[]`, `access`, `terms`, `parent`, `eligibility`,
     `languages`, each with per-field provenance and a DATE, as attrs or
     first-class. Whether `offers[]` (the tok-sen fix) and `holds[]` are one
     array or two.

  C. THE SOURCE GENRE — a roster importer, not a crawl: which rosters exist
     for CM/CR, who maintains each, what licence, what each yields. MEASURE
     BEFORE BUILD (source-scout pattern): count the MFA consular list, the
     mosque list, the foundation registry, before writing a line.

  D. THE EVIDENCE BAR — access rules come only from the institution's own
     words or a roster, never inferred; every rule carries `asOf`. The
     reader must be able to see the date.

  E. THE INDEX — how a HOLDING is searched: "Tai Khün manuscripts" must land
     on EFEO and Payap; "Dutch passport" on the honorary consul. That is
     vocabulary.json (Phase 1 of go/cure) fed a second kind of term.

  F. THE READER FACE — an institution page answers "may I, and how", not
     "is it open now". Different card, different tests, different first line.

  G. SIZE OF THE FIRST TRANCHE — the ~30 that a resident scholar would name
     from memory (EFEO, Payap Archives, National Archives CM, CMU Social
     Research Institute, the Foreign Cemetery, the consulates, the mosques,
     the churches, INTG, the Quaker meeting, Chabad, the Gymkhana Club) vs
     the full roster pull.

None of A–G is decided. They are Nan's.
