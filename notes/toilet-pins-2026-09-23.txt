FORKS

1 · Pins at a centre — 1,191 walk-in, 35 customer rows
  (a) leave. No work. Worst case: a reader in a tambon walks to a wat the
      list puts at 120 m and finds it 2 km off.
  (b) off the toilet list: bake() skips rows pinned at a postcode, tambon
      or amphoe centre. Landmark- and street-placed rows and the 357 block
      wats stay. The fleet search reads the baked file, so its 364 lines
      follow. One condition in toilets_layer.py; 1,191 wats leave the list
      and keep their pages.
  (c) keep, labelled ≈ with the method's typical error ("within about
      2 km"). Distance-only sort stays, so the row still sorts at a
      distance it does not have. A label in both languages and one column.
  (d) drop every non-exact row. Also loses the 357 block wats, 8 of them
      in the 20 nearest Tha Phae Gate.
