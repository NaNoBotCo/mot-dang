THE FAULT — RARITY IS NOT WEIGHED
  Three words. buy matched 261 rows, martin 4, guitar 4. No row matched all
  three, so the engine dropped to partial and kept every row matching any one
  of them. Every one of those rows has the same coverage (1 of 3). They then
  sort by score, and score does not know that a word matching 4 rows out of
  22,351 says a thousand times more about what the reader wants than a word
  matching 261.

  The rare word IS the question. "guitar" is the question. "buy" is grammar.
  Ranked by rarity, the eight shops are the first eight rows and Michael
  never sees a Cafe Amazon.

  This is the same organism as the parking bug and a different mechanism:
  there, a constraint word was eaten before matching; here, the words all
  survive and are then weighed as equals.

SECOND FAULT — "buy" SHOULD NOT MATCH 261 RESTAURANTS
  Not yet pinned. Two candidates, and I have not proved either:
    - the loose/near-spelling tier reaching buy -> pui (Khao Tom Pui, Leng
      Doi Pui, Mod Khaotom Pui 2, Pom Pui Big Size all rank in this pile,
      which is not a coincidence worth ignoring)
    - "Instruments — buy & rent" is a real shelf label, so buy is a genuine
      index token; but that would give ~30 rows, not 261.
  Whichever it is, buy/place/best are query scaffolding, not content.
