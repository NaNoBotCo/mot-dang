OVERTURE — WHAT THE FOLD DID
Built 2026-09-07 · Nan: "a and b" · go/overture


WHAT WAS ASKED
--------------
Fork A: use Overture Places to fill contact facts on records Mot Dang already
holds. Fork B: admit the places it holds that Mot Dang does not.


WHAT IT IS
----------
Overture Maps Places, release 2026-08-19.0, CDLA-Permissive 2.0, off a public
S3 bucket with no account. In Chiang Mai and Chiang Rai it is 96,870 rows and
**99% of them come from Meta** — they are Facebook business pages, carrying the
phone and address the page itself states.

That matters because of the 9/6 directive. OSM is not first truth; a place
describes itself on Facebook; and the blocker was never policy, it was
mechanism — five logged-out fetches of facebook.com gave no fact, and dressing
a script as a browser to get one is off-limits. This is the same fact arriving
through an open front door.

It was parked on 8/19 as "GB-scale parquet, not stdlib — its own decision".
Both halves are gone: duckdb reads the remote file in place and takes only the
rows it wants. The extract is 33 seconds.

WHAT IT REFUSES TO DO, AND THE MEASUREMENTS BEHIND EACH REFUSAL
---------------------------------------------------------------
1. IT NEVER MATCHES ON A NAME ALONE.
   Over 3,072 records with a surveyed pin whose name matches exactly one
   Overture row in the province: median 32 m, 64% inside 100 m, **p90 21 km,
   p95 134 km**. The middle says the two datasets agree; the tail says name
   equality across a province is routinely two different shops. So identity is
   name AND within 300 m.

2. IT NEVER MOVES A PIN.
   595 records still have no coordinates and Overture appears to offer some.
   Under the strictest rule available — unique name, and the point inside the
   อำเภอ our own address names — 555 candidates came out at median 62 m but
   **p90 5,551 m**, above the ±3,000 m this repo publishes at. Requiring
   confidence >= 0.8 gave p90 3,896 m. Still over. 85 records could have had a
   pin tonight and about a quarter of them would have been the wrong place, so
   they keep their honest absence.

3. IT NEVER OVERWRITES. Only empty fields are filled. Survey, register,
   curated correction and an owner's claim all outrank it and all run later.

4. IT NEVER SETTLES A NEAR-MISS. 6,897 rows stood within 25 m of a record we
   hold, on the same shelf, under a name the match rule did not recognise.
   Sampling found both kinds — real duplicates under two spellings (บ้านกาแฟ
   and "Ban Coffee", 4 m apart) and real neighbours (a sushi bar 11 m from an
   MK). A join cannot tell those apart, so they went to
   cache/overture_review_cm.txt and _cr.txt for a person. 25 m and not 50 m
   because 50 m of a Chiang Mai soi is several shops.

5. IT IS NEVER GIVEABLE TO OPENSTREETMAP. The source type is `directory`, so
   provenance.py classes it FORBIDDEN and the give-back gate refuses it
   without being told. Correct: CDLA-Permissive is not ODbL and Meta's rows
   are not ours to relicense. The cost was counted before it was taken — on
   2026-09-07 no record in the catalogue had a giveable or licence-waiting
   field, so nothing was lost.


WHAT IT COSTS
-------------
- **Hours coverage falls from 17% to 5%.** Overture carries no opening hours
  at all, so 63,170 records arrive without any and dilute the fraction. The
  4,402 records that state hours still state them. Nothing was lost; the
  denominator grew.
- **The catalogue is 3.4x bigger.** Every per-record cost multiplies: build
  time, page count, the search index, the assistant's vector index.
- **It is Facebook's view.** A closed shop keeps its page. An owner's house
  can be a business location. 30% of rows carry a confidence below 0.5 and
  were refused, but confidence is Overture's guess, not a survey.
- **6% of rows could not be filed** — no category we map, or none at all.
  They enriched but were not admitted.

One test elsewhere was rewritten, not weakened: tests/test_kinds.py asserted
that more than 500 register-described wats are rescued from noindex by the
kind-aware gate. Enrichment gave 238 of those wats an ADDRESS, so they now
clear even the old shop-shaped gate and leave the rescued population BY GETTING
BETTER. A count over a moving corpus drifts on its own — the same trap as the
romaniser's corpus floor. It now asserts the property that does not drift:
every register-described wat that fails the shop-shaped gate is rescued.
173 of 173.
