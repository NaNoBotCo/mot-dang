NOT PUBLISHED, AND WHY — data/search_segdict.txt
-------------------------------------------------------------------

The 40,408-word segmentation dictionary was on the list and did not go.
I cannot name its source.

Its own header says "words present in the corpus". Its contents disagree:
it holds ก กา (a grammar term), ก.พ. and two dozen other abbreviations,
and whole sentences — ค่ายมวยและสนามมวยมีชั้นมวยไทยของตัวเองแล้ว,
(มีชั้นเรียนทำอาหารไทยของตัวเองแล้ว). Those are not corpus words.

Nothing in the repo generates it. It arrived in commit 5447a3397e, whose
subject is "WO-27 door 6b: fold the Treasury condominium register as
records" — a commit about condominiums. A file with no generator, no
stated source and a header contradicted by its contents cannot be given a
licence, so it stays home until you say otherwise.

TWO THINGS FOUND ON THE WAY
-------------------------------------------------------------------

A TRADE COMPOUND MUST NEVER ENTER THE SEGMENTER. The first Space build fed
the 1,782 trade terms to translit's word list, and ตู้น้ำดื่มหยอดเหรียญ came
out "tunamduemyotrian" — one unbroken run. A maximal-matching segmenter
that knows the whole compound will never break it into the four words a
reader says. The lexicon is for reading, not for cutting. Nothing on the
site does this today; it is a trap for whoever wires the two together next.

OVERTURE IS CLASSED "forbidden" BY provenance.py. source_class() returns
FORBIDDEN for any source whose type is "directory", and Overture's type is
directory — so all 473,447 Overture field stamps, which is nearly the whole
provenance table, are marked never-exportable. Overture is CDLA-Permissive
2.0. If that classification is wrong the OSM give-back gate (go/osm-gate)
is holding back far more than the 3,303 it thinks it is. Not touched.
