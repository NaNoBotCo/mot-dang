hero folder + home.css/home.js — minutes, not the 2 h 18 walk.

2. THE PAGE — six scenes, one scroll
================================================================================

Every scene is one screen tall on a phone. The picture behind each scene
moves slower than the words (parallax). Under prefers-reduced-motion or
Save-Data the pictures stand still and load at 800 px.

 0  THE MOMENT      Full-bleed picture chosen by the hour and the season
                    (dawn · day · dusk · night; a festival week takes its own).
                    Wordmark. The box. One line under it, no sentence:
                    the Thai clock · the day's colour as a dot · the moon ·
                    temp · PM. Every mark opens something; nothing is labelled
                    until อา.

 1  ตอนนี้ NOW      Counters that move: places open now (the curve behind),
                    "next" as hours-away (the next market, the next film, the
                    next event within reach), a countdown to the nearest
                    festival or wan phra. A live clock in Thai and B.E.

 2  ใกล้ๆ NEAR      City chosen for the reader, not asked. Coarse position
                    picks Chiang Mai or Chiang Rai; the map postcard opens
                    there. One tap (the existing MDLOC door) refines to the
                    nine cells: nearest open food, nearest toilet, nearest
                    pharmacy, nearest clean-air room when PM is high. Straight
                    lines, and it says so.

 3  ฤกษ์ THE DAY    One card, quiet: the colour, the planet, the Buddha
                    posture, the moon, the lucky pair. A tap opens the
                    horoscope (the live wheels already built), the shrines,
                    the merit page, the katha. Jupiter's moons as tonight's
                    small drawing when the sky is clear.

 4  เจาะลึก DEEP    The minisites as full-bleed picture tiles: the Loop, Muay
                    Thai, Hand Poke, Amulet Essentials, the Atlas… read from
                    fleet.json so a new site appears here on its next build.
                    This is the shelf that grows.

 5  THE DOOR        The two cities with their counts, ทุกชั้น ทุกหมวด, the
                    glyph footer, the picture credits.

Predicting the need — a rule table, not a model. Hour × weekday × conditions
picks which scene leads and which card sits first:
  05–08   alms, wan phra, the morning markets
  11–13   open-now food first
  PM2.5 over the PCD line   clean-air rooms first, the number in red
  rain in the forecast      indoor shelves first
  Saturday evening          Wualai; Sunday   Tha Phae walking street
  Songkran · Yi Peng · Loy Krathong week   scene 0 becomes the festival
  22:00 on                  pharmacies, 24-hour, the red phone
Deterministic, baked as JSON, picked by the reader's clock; testable in
tests/test_home.py with a frozen clock.

Words on the page: the nine stay. Titles and aria-labels carry the names;
อา prints them. Counts computed at build time, never typed. No claim about
what the page does not do.

3. TECH
================================================================================

home_layer.py (new)         the three functions build.py already calls
docs/home.css, home.js      one sheet, one script, no framework, no request
                            beyond the pictures and, on the tap, nine cells
docs/site/hero/             AVIF + JPEG at 800 / 1600 / 2400, srcset
Parallax                    translate3d on scroll under requestAnimationFrame;
                            off under reduced-motion and Save-Data
Payload                     one inline JSON as the band does now: 45 days of
                            almanac, 21 days of events, both forecasts, the
                            picture roster, the rule table
Coarse geo                  the Worker stamps cf.city/lat/lon/timezone into
                            one response header on "/" ; home.js reads it;
                            nothing stored, nothing in the URL
First paint budget          measured and printed by the test, not capped
Accessibility               scrims under every word on a picture, 4.5:1
                            checked at build; อา and the language control as
                            today; works with no script (the form submits,
                            the tiles are links)

Effort: two sessions. Hero re-fetch and conversion ~2 h of machine time.
Publish: homepage-only rclone, minutes; rollback is one command.
