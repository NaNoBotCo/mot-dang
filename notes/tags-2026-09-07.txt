🏷  TAGGING — WO-76
    built 2026-09-07 · NOT deployed · nothing was built or published from this tree

WHAT YOU ASKED FOR
    Tag everything, more aggressively than seems enough, and open many more ways
    to find a listing through its tags. And: "stub" should be a tag.

THE NUMBER THAT MADE THE CASE
    Before:   5,606 of 88,161 records carried a tag        6.4%
    After:   88,159 of 88,161                            100.0%
    Two records carry nothing: they hold a name and no field at all.

    Vocabulary:    76 tags  ->  1,356      (88 written by hand, 1,174 mined,
                                            94 chains)
    Tags per record: 0.1  ->  3.3 mean, 3 median, 24 at most
    Pages:        1,841 tag pages · 1,765 tag x shelf pages · 17 family pages

WHY IT WAS 6.4%
    Not because the rules were wrong. The 76 hand-written rules read fields like
    attrs.cuisine, which 2,125 records carry. The corpus is 88,161 records over
    288 attrs keys. The vocabulary was hand-sized and the corpus was not.

    The Overture fold sharpened it. 63,170 of the new records carry
    attrs.overtureCategory — 681 distinct words — and nothing read that field.
    18,730 of them stand on a shelf with NO sub-shelf (13,668 on food alone), so
    that word was the only fine grain they had, and they were findable by name
    and by nothing else.

WHAT WAS BUILT

  1. MINED TAGS — tags_mine.py (new)
     What _brand_tags already did for attrs.brand, now done for every
     categorical field: read the field's own values off the records, mint one
     tag per value that clears a floor. Nothing typed on, one stated rule per
     tag, and the vocabulary grows the day a crawl meets a new word.

         trade      393   attrs.tradeTags — the vernacular off the sign
                          (ซักผ้าหยอดเหรียญ, ปะยางมอเตอร์ไซค์, ตอกเส้น), floor 3
         kind       380   attrs.overtureCategory, floor 8
         area       321   amphoe · tambon · street · postcode, floor 5–8
         cuisine     55   every cuisine value past the 34 written by hand
         medical     35   facilityType + specialty
         school      21 · comfort 16 · wat 5

  2. "STUB", and the rest of the state family — 9 tags on a new `field` rule
     kind that reads which fields the RECORD holds, not what the place is:

         stub                8,554 in CM   one field or none of eight
         name-only              94         no field at all
         no-pin              5,363
         no-contact         10,467         no phone, site, page or email held
         has-phone          44,007 · has-facebook 48,830 · has-website 15,743
         has-hours           4,003 · multi-source 5,310

     Every one of those pages says in its lede that it describes the entry and
     not the shop, and that a place with no hours held is not a place that is
     shut.

  3. HOURS ARE NOW READ, not just ticked. A small opening_hours parser (";" and
     newline rules, 2- and 3-letter days, past-midnight closes). open-late went
     from 28 records to 1,770 because it had only ever read an owner's tick.
     New: open-sunday 3,020 · open-monday 3,841 · open-early 1,156.

  4. DISCOVERY — four surfaces, all with counts on the face

     a. "These are also" on every tag page — the tags that tag's places most
        often ALSO carry, ranked by LIFT rather than count, each chip a link to
        search.html?tag=a&tag=b, which is already a real AND filter. 1,101 tags
        have one.

            motorbike repair -> oil change (130) · puncture repair (143)
            dental work      -> fillings (34) · braces (50) · extractions (8)
            coin laundry     -> coin washing machines (67) · 24-hour laundry (24)
            vegan            -> vegetarian (59) · gluten-free (6) · organic (5)

        Ranked by count instead, every rail on the site read "also: has a phone
        number (44,007)". Lift asks the better question and the state family is
        kept off the rails entirely.

     b. "Narrow this shelf" on every shelf page — the tags most carried by the
        records on that shelf, linking to the tag x shelf page where one exists.
        26,984 rows of food is not a list anyone reads; coffee shop (2,498),
        Thai restaurant (1,504), open late (594) is a set of doors.

     c. Family rail on every tag page — the siblings, commonest first, for when
        this tag was nearly the right word.

     d. /tags.html rebuilt for the scale, plus one page per family
        (/tags/trade.html and so on) with a type-to-narrow box. 1,356 tags do
        not fit on one page and were not made to.

  5. PILLS ARE ORDERED RAREST FIRST. A café now carries `coffee shop` (3,679)
     and `coin laundry` (200) at once and only one of those is why anyone would
     land on it — the same lesson the guitar bug taught ranking. Capped at 14
     with the rest folded.

FORKS — yours

  F1  NINE OVERTURE WORDS ARE SKIPPED as pure shelf-restatements: restaurant,
      food, eat_and_drink, shopping, accommodation, education,
      professional_services, business_to_business, structure_and_geography.
      `restaurant` alone is 7,507 records and a tag page for it duplicates
      /cm/food/. Cost of minting them: nine more big pages that say what the
      shelf already says. Cost of skipping: those 7,507 lose one tag each.
      No expiry — it is one line in data/tags.json either way.

  F2  THE STATE FAMILY IS PUBLIC. `stub`, `no-pin` and `no-contact` each get
      their own page, listing by name the 8,554 / 5,363 / 10,467 records where
      this catalogue holds least. That is a true list, it is useful (it is the
      work queue, and it is the door an owner walks in through), and it is also
      the thinnest part of the directory with a page of its own and a place in
      the sitemap. The alternative is to assign the tags — so search and the
      filters still use them — and draw no page. Your call, not mine.
      Cost either way: nothing to rebuild, one flag.

  F3  934 MINED TAGS HAVE NO HAND-WRITTEN LABEL. 348 are English words the
      builder de-snakes acceptably ("Auto parts and supply store"). 586 are
      Thai and now carry their RTGS reading as the English half.
      [Amended 2026-09-08. This fork used to say those 586 rendered Thai-only,
      "because translit.py's rule says a reading never fills an English name
      slot". Nobody wrote that rule — see go/rules-cut. A tag is not a place
      and a tag's English half is not nameEn, so no shop is being given a name
      it did not choose, and an English-mode reader can now read, type and
      remember the chip.]
      What is left of the fork: whether you want hand-written labels over the
      readings. I labelled 304; `python3 tags_mine.py -v` prints the rest,
      commonest first. No expiry.

  F4  THE FLOORS. trade 3, kind 8, area 5, brand 5. Lower floors mean more
      tags and more pages: at trade 3 there are 393 trade tags; the field holds
      1,782 distinct words, so a floor of 1 would be ~1,800. You said more
      aggressive than seems enough — this is where that dial is.

  F5  THREE MORE DOORS I DID NOT BUILD, because each touches the search JS or
      the home page and both are being worked in this tree tonight:
        · a zero-result search offering the nearest tags instead of nothing;
        · a tag rail on /here.html;
