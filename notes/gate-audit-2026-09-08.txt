WHAT I FOUND, IN ONE LINE

Three test files carry numbers that can fail a build. All three are UNTRACKED
— none has ever been committed. NINE numbers remained after the 10 MB went
(one of them, a cap of 30, was not even a named constant — it sat inline in an
assertion, which is why the first count in this note said eight). Not one of
them was put to you.

They are not all the same kind of thing, and saying so matters more than the
count:


§1  NUMBERS ENFORCING SOMETHING YOU DID SAY

tests/test_page_weight.py — born 2026-09-02, out of WO-52.

The gate exists because of a real measurement: 33 repeated blocks were
5,547,155 of the site's 7,752,480 prose word-instances; 85% of the words on a
place page were blocks repeated site-wide; the median place page ran 350 words
of prose of which 51 were about the place. And "reduce the words, everywhere,
always" is yours, said many times, and Michael's "if you need to explain
something using words, you're fucking up" is the same instruction from the
other side.

So the DIRECTION is yours. Every number in it is a session's:

    (inline)                 =  30     prose repeated on 500+ pages   GONE
    MAX_MEDIAN_RECORD_WORDS  =  60     prose words on a place page    GONE
    MAX_MEDIAN_TOTAL_WORDS   = 180     everything visible             GONE
    MAX_LEAD_IN_WORDS        =  25     between the <h1> and the first fact
    MAX_HEADER_OPEN_LINKS    =   6     links in the masthead
    MAX_HOME_OPEN_LINKS      =  36     links on the front page

The file says outright where they came from: "Each is set well clear of where
WO-52 landed the site (median 101 words, 10 words of lead-in)." That is the
same recipe as the 10 MB — measure where we happen to stand, draw the line
above it, and every later session inherits a number nobody chose.

The nav pair (6 and 36) encode WO-65, where the instruction WAS yours —
"Remove all other forms of navigation, other than what I'm describing" — but
six and thirty-six are still inventions.

AND ONE OF THEM HAS ALREADY MISFIRED. Check 2 used to cap all visible words at
140 as a stand-in for "boilerplate prose". A bearings line plus five nearest
places is about 40 words that DIFFER on every one of 25,800 pages — the
opposite of the disease the gate was built for — and the proxy read 159 and
failed the build. The proxy was fixed; the episode is the argument.


§2  A NUMBER ENFORCING NOTHING YOU SAID — REMOVED 2026-09-08

tests/test_kinds.py

    RESIDUE_MAX = 40    records the kind rules cannot place        GONE

Its own comment: "A judgement call, written down: how much unplaced residue is
honest. 16 today." Honest about being a judgement, and it was a session's
judgement, not yours — 40 was chosen because 16 was the number that day. You
said remove it, and it is removed; the count is reported instead.


§3  TWO NUMBERS THAT ARE METHOD, NOT RULE

    REPEAT_THRESHOLD = 500    a block on this many pages counts as furniture
    SAMPLE           = 400    pages sampled per run

These do not cap anything; they define what is being measured and how much of
it. They still encode a judgement — 500 is somebody's idea of where a repeated
block stops being writing and starts being wallpaper — but a reader is never
told about them and no work is blocked by them directly.
