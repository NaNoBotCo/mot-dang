A VIDEO ON THE PLACE PAGE — what the film@ door needs from Mot Dang
===================================================================
2026-09-22 · proposal, not built · from the capture project

WHAT ARRIVES
Shop owners mail a clip of their own shop to film@motdang.net. The ear stores
it; `capture contrib` files it under raw/<session>/broll/ with the sender's
consent lines and their PromptPay id, and writes raw/<session>/contrib/<id>.json
carrying `motdang_place_id` when the shop is one Mot Dang already lists.

WHAT THE OFFER SAYS
"We put it on your shop's page on Mot Dang for free." That page does not take
a video today.

THE BLOCK
One optional block on a place page: a <video> with poster, muted, plays on
tap, under the photos. Source of truth: a small file the capture pipeline
writes, data/place_videos.json — {place_id: {mp4, poster, credit, consent}} —
read at build time like citylife_links.json is. Consent gate on the build:
only rows whose consent has tier2_editorial true are rendered; a face-visible
false row uses the blurred render. The build refuses a row with no consent
field at all.

HOSTING
room.mp4 renders are 1080p H.264; a 30-second clip is 8–15 MB. R2 bucket
`fleet-video`, public read via the existing motdang Worker, path
/v/<place_id>/<clip>.mp4. Poster is a JPEG frame the pipeline writes.

COST TO BUILD
Half a day: the JSON reader in build.py, the block in the place template, the
R2 upload step in the capture pipeline's export, and one test that a row with
no consent never renders.

NOT DECIDED
The share paid when a clip earns (hers). Whether the block also runs on the
front page's SOON list when a clip is new.
