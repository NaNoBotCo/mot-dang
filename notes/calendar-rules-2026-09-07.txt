YOUR DECISIONS 2026-09-07

Storage    Rules are records.  Queryable.  Not on the homepage.
           Functional fields only — a field unrelated to the record does not
           get a column.  No prose in the data.

Publishing Rules stay in the background.  No rule is stated to the public
           unless law requires it.

Gaps       A record missing data is stored and backfilled as data arrives.
           Background job.  Reported to you, to nobody else.  Applies to
           every rule, not only these.

Reader     Fix the date question.  "What is on 22-26 Nov" and "what is open
           now" are different questions; the site answers neither.


ONE OPEN QUESTION

Loi Krathong, Yi Peng and wan phra fall on the full moon or a lunar quarter.
The date is arithmetic and can be computed years ahead.  The code refuses to
compute it and will only take a date printed in an official announcement.  It
has found none, so the festival calendar is empty.

Compute the dates, or keep waiting?

Cost of computing: the astronomical full moon is exact, but the city sets its
own dates for lantern releases and road closures, and those can land a day
either side.  A computed date is right about the moon and can be wrong about
the event.

Cost of waiting: what is there now, which is nothing.
