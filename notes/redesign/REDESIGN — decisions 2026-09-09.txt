WHAT THE FONT WEIGHS, measured 2026-09-09 against jsDelivr:
  Phosphor regular  .woff2  147,380 bytes (144 kB)   1,530 icons
                    .woff   488,716 bytes (477 kB)
  style.css                  78,131 bytes ( 76 kB)
  our two sprites, inline:   20,021 bytes ( 20 kB)   122 marks
                             (build.ICON_SPRITE 6,821 + glyphs.py drawings 13,200)
So Phosphor as shipped costs 220 kB before an icon draws, for 1,530 icons of
which the site uses 113. Two levers, both worth taking on a rural connection:
subset the font to the 113 (the CSS goes with it, since it is one rule per
icon), and self-host rather than hit a CDN.

WHAT AN ICON FONT NEEDS IN ORDER TO BE ACCESSIBLE
· The glyph is a private-use codepoint drawn by ::before. If the font does not
  arrive, it renders as a blank or a tofu box, silently. So no control may
  depend on its icon to be understood: every one carries a real text label, or
  an aria-label when the label is visually hidden.
· The <i> is aria-hidden="true", always. A screen reader must never read the
  codepoint.
· font-display: block, not swap — a fallback glyph in a text face is noise.
· The nine marks that stay ours are inline SVG in the sprite, so they draw
  with no network at all. The ant among them: the brand never depends on a
  font request.

THE PALETTE — both, and the numbers that hold it up
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Ratios are WCAG 2.1: 4.5:1 for text under 18.5px, 3:1 for a graphic or a
boundary that carries meaning.

LIGHT, the spec's own values:
  ink   #211d18 on paper #faf8f2   15.77:1   text        PASS
  red   #c62f22 on paper           5.16:1    text        PASS
  white #ffffff on red   #c62f22   5.48:1    button      PASS
  hairline #d8d2c4 on paper        1.42:1    boundary    FAIL
  selected #f0e9dc on paper        1.14:1    state       FAIL

DARK, the counterpart:
  ink   #f0ece3 on ground #14120f  15.86:1   text        PASS
  meta  #b3aa99 on ground          8.13:1    text        PASS
  red   #ff6b5c on ground          6.69:1    text        PASS
  ground#14120f on red   #ff6b5c   6.69:1    button      PASS

ONE RED CANNOT SERVE BOTH — measured:
  #ff6b5c on paper   2.63:1  FAIL     #c62f22 on the dark ground  3.41:1  FAIL
So light and dark each take their own red. The one value that clears 3:1 as a
GRAPHIC on both is the logo's own #e0524d — 3.61:1 on paper, 4.88:1 on the
dark ground — which is why the raster logo fallbacks are drawn in it.

THREE THINGS THE SPEC LEAVES BELOW THE LINE, and the fix for each:
· THE META LINE. The spec sets counts and meta at "neutral-500". At 12–13px
  that is body text, and the common neutral-500s do not clear it: #737373 is
  4.46:1, #8a8578 is 3.46:1. #5c574c gives 6.77:1 and still reads as quiet.
· THE HAIRLINE. #d8d2c4 is 1.42:1 — decoration, not a boundary. If the rule
  is the only thing separating one row from the next it has to be seen:
  #928e85 is the first warm value that reaches 3:1 (3.08). If the rows are
  told apart some other way, the pale rule can stay as ornament.
· THE SELECTED STATE. No warm tint of the paper reaches 3:1 — #dfd0b0, already
  darker than the spec's, is 1.43:1. So selection cannot be carried by fill.
  It needs a second cue that survives both low vision and a monochrome screen:
  a rule down the leading edge in the accent, plus aria-pressed / aria-current
  so it is announced rather than merely seen.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
UNIVERSAL ACCESSIBILITY — what the spec does not yet say
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The spec covers hit targets, the easy-read mode, aria-labels on icons and a
no-JS path. These are the gaps.

· THE TWO LANGUAGES NEED lang. Thai and English sit in the same line all over
  this site. Without lang="th" / lang="en" on each half, a screen reader says
  the English in Thai phonemes and the Thai in English ones — the bilingual
  page is the one that most needs the attribute, and the spec never mentions
  it. Every paired string carries it; <html lang> is the majority language of
  the page, not of the reader's toggle.
· FOCUS HAS TO BE VISIBLE. No ratio is given for a focus ring. Two-pixel ring
  plus a two-pixel offset, in the ink rather than the accent — #211d18 on
  paper is 15.77:1, so it is visible against the selected fill and the paper
  alike, which the red at 5.16:1 is not on every ground it lands on.
· ZOOM AND REFLOW. 400% zoom at 1280px wide must not produce a horizontal
  scrollbar. The 560px centre column and the one-column mobile layout both
  survive it; the horizontally-scrolling filter chip rail is the one place to
  check, since a chip rail and a reflowed page fight each other.
· TARGET SIZE. The spec's 44px is the AA figure and the right one. Two places
  it has to be measured rather than assumed: the language segmented control,
  three targets in one row, and the phone button that sits inside a tappable
  row — a target inside a target needs its own 44px and a gap, or the wrong
  one fires.
· THE ONE THING NO ICON MAY CARRY ALONE is the emergency row. A siren glyph
  with no word is not a hotline. Numbers are exempt from the word budget, and
  1669 next to a red phone is the row: the numeral does the work.
· MOTION. The masthead has a rotate on hover today. prefers-reduced-motion
  turns every transform and transition off, not down.
· THE FORM. The search box needs a real <label>, visually hidden if the
  design wants no visible word; a placeholder is not a name. The mic button
  is a separate control with its own label, never the box's.
· AND THE ONE THE WORD BUDGET THREATENS. Nine visible words is a design goal,
  not an accessibility one. Icons that lose their labels lose them for
  everyone who does not already know the site. The budget holds as long as
  every glyph keeps its accessible name and its tooltip, which is what the
  spec says — it just has to survive the build.

THE LOGO — what was built from the file, and why
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Source: motdang-logo.svg, as given — 720×720, a neon-line ant on a dark navy
ground, in a frame, over มดแดง and motdang.net.

At 40px the frame, the wordmark and the URL are a smudge; the badge is drawn
for a card, not for a tab or a masthead. So the ant is lifted out of it — the
same drawing, the same geometry, nothing redrawn — and squared:

  assets/logo/motdang-logo.svg        her file, untouched
  assets/logo/motdang-mark.svg        the ant, transparent, square viewBox,
                                      stroke="currentColor" — it takes the
                                      colour of whatever it is placed on
  assets/logo/motdang-favicon.svg     transparent, and carrying its own
                                      light/dark rule, because currentColor
                                      has nothing to inherit inside a tab
  assets/logo/motdang-mark-{512,192,180,32}.png   raster fallbacks in #e0524d,
                                      the one red that clears 3:1 on both

STROKE WEIGHT — Nan, 2026-09-09: "lines on the logo SVG (top left) should be 4×
heavier. Favicon should be visually similar." Every stroke in the drawing is
multiplied by 4 (the 5/4/3.5 of her badge become 20/16/14), and the favicon
carries the same weight, so the tab and the masthead are one mark. The square
grew by half a stroke width so the heavier line cannot clip its own box —
checked on the 512px render: the ink stops at 82/18 and 429/495 inside 512.
One weight now serves every size; the separate 2.2× small file is gone.

Wired in build.py:
  · LOGO_MARK reads assets/logo/motdang-mark.svg — one file holds the drawing.
  · The masthead draws LOGO_MARK instead of ANT_MARK.
  · .logomark loses background:var(--ant), its border and its inset shadow.
    It was a filled red tile with a paper-coloured ant inside; it is now the
    mark alone, at colour var(--ant), filling its 62px square.
  · The favicon stops being the 🐜 emoji in a data URI — which was the exact
    thing glyphs.py was written to end, an emoji being whatever the reader's
    phone decides it is — and becomes the SVG, with a 32px PNG and a 180px
    apple-touch icon behind it.
  · assets/logo/ is copied to docs/logo/ in the same block as wat.svg.

Left alone: ANT_MARK is still defined and is now unused. Deleting it is a
separate, deliberate change.

Still open, one thing: at 16px no version of this ant reads as an ant — the
legs and feelers merge whatever the stroke weight, and 4× merges them sooner
than 2.2× did. What 4× buys is 32px and up, where the mark now reads at a
glance. A 16px drawing that is recognisably an ant would be a new drawing.
