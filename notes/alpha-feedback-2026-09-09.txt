96,372 pages, not deployed to R2.


WHAT IT IS

Two thumbs, bottom left of every page. Thumb up is one tap and it is sent.
Thumb down opens a box: what went wrong, and up to three screenshots.

Each report carries what the tester could not have typed:

  the address, and the query in it        what the search box held
  how many rows the page actually had     how many survived the filters
  where the map was pointing, and zoom    whether the tiles ever arrived
  every JS error since the page loaded    every request that came back wrong
  the last dozen taps, timed              build date, screen size, language
  the connection type                     whether the offline worker was on

"Search is bad" becomes "search for กีตาร์ returned 200 rows on the 9 Sept
build, none of them a guitar shop, with a 404 from the index behind it".

A send that does not land is kept on the phone and goes with the next page the
tester opens. They are told which of the two happened.


WHO SEES IT

Only a browser that has been handed a tester name, once, by a link:

    https://motdang.net/?tester=beer
    https://motdang.net/?tester=michael
    https://motdang.net/?tester=nan

The name is taken out of the address bar straight away, so a tester who shares
a URL does not pass their name on. Every other reader gets the site with
nothing added: the script exits before it touches the page.

Set "audience": "everyone" in data/alpha.json for a day of open testing.


WHERE THE REPORTS GO

Email, one per report, to 530kings@proton.me — with the screenshots attached
when they are small enough to attach, and the R2 key named when they are not.
That is the read path; no dashboard, no pull step, no login.

The second copy is in the worker's KV for 180 days:

    python3 importers/sync_alpha.py
        → data/alpha_reports.json + notes/alpha-reports.txt (a week in one sitting)

Screenshots live in the private R2 bucket the listing photos already use
(alpha/<report id>/1.jpg). Nothing serves that bucket.


TURNING IT OFF

data/alpha.json, "on": false — or delete the file. Then no page mentions
alpha.js, the file is not written, and nothing is loaded, hidden or listening.
tests/test_alpha.py checks that against the built tree, both ways.

"until": "2026-12-31" turns it off by itself and the build says so. Move the
date to keep it.

At beta, gone completely is four deletions:
    alpha_layer.py · data/alpha.json · importers/sync_alpha.py · tests/test_alpha.py
    the import and the one tag line in build.py page()
    the two marked blocks in worker/worker.js
    (and `wrangler kv key delete --remote --prefix alpha:` if the reports have
     served their purpose)
