ONE THING TO KNOW BEFORE THE NEXT BUILD
------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUILD_DATE (build.py:80) is hand-maintained and reads 2026-09-05 while
today is the 6th. The events cell counts "today" from that constant,
the same one the rest of the site uses — so on a build day where the
constant was not bumped, the line says "on today" about yesterday. That
is not new and it is not mine, but a cell whose whole job is NOW is where
it will finally show. Bump it, or key the count to the real build day.
