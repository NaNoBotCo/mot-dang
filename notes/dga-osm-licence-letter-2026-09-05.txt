Dear Digital Government Development Agency,

I build Mot Dang (motdang.net), an open, Thai-first city directory for Chiang
Mai and Chiang Rai. It is free to read and publishes its whole corpus for
reuse.

The directory currently holds 6,150 records drawn from datasets on data.go.th
— ONAB's temple register, OBEC's school register, the Ministry of Education's
private-school licence register, CITIZENinfo, and others. Every record states
its source, as the licence requires.

I would like to contribute the NAMES and ADDRESSES from those datasets to
OpenStreetMap, the free editable map whose database is used by a great many
other maps worldwide. OpenStreetMap publishes under the Open Database License
(ODbL), and gives credit at the level of the SOURCE — a named entry on the
project's contributors page — rather than record by record.

I have read the Terms and Conditions for DGA Open Government License, version
1.0 of 10 June 2021, and I understand them to say:

  1. The rights granted are non-exclusive, without limitation of territory or
     period, requiring no further authorization and no fee.
  2. A user may "usefully process the Data by means of ... gathering the Data
     therefrom together with other data."
  3. There is no share-alike condition and no restriction on redistribution
     under a different licence.
  4. The standing obligation is attribution, and where the producing
     organization cannot be identified individually the Terms themselves
     prescribe a blanket reference: "The right to Use the Data is subject to
     the terms and conditions for DGA Open Government License."

May I ask you to confirm in writing that this reading is correct, and that DGA
has no objection to data published under this licence being incorporated into
OpenStreetMap under ODbL with attribution given at source level. If any
individual publishing agency must be asked separately, I would be grateful to
be told which, so I can write to them in turn.

Your reply would be attached to the OpenStreetMap import documentation so that
the permission is publicly checkable. No data will be uploaded before we hear
from you.

With respect,
[name]
Mot Dang · motdang.net
