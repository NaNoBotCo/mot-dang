THE WORD IS TWO WORDS, AND THAT IS THE WHOLE TRAP

waiting for the bus. The remaining 2 are the 2 shops already held. Overture
adds 13 more in-corpus hits and 11 of those are homestays and cottages.

So OSM is finished on this trade: a re-crawl returns the two records already
on the shelf. And reading the name off the open corpus fills the kratom shelf
with holiday cottages.

THE REVIEWS — SHE SAID READ THEM
Nan, 2026-09-21: "if the reviews mention kratom, please add it." That narrows
the refusal rather than lifting it. `offers_from_reviews()` runs before
`_strip_refused()` and returns product keys and a mention count. No wording,
author, date, rating or link is kept, and none reaches an entry, a record or a
page. The claim lands in attrs.offers, which already exists and is already
indexed for search, as via "visitor-review" — the weakest source in OFFER_VIA,
printing as "จากรีวิวของผู้ไปใช้บริการ / from a visitor's review".

sub is what a place IS and its own sign says that; a customer writing "they
have kratom too" is evidence of what is on sale. So a shop that already earned
the sub from its own name takes no offer row.

The guard reads the clause around the word for ไหม · มั้ย · หรือเปล่า · ป่าว ·

A BUG FOUND ON THE WAY, AND FIXED — 29 SHOPS
harvest_weedth.py HTML-unescaped the ld+json text and then parsed it. A
reviewer typing a double quote is written &quot; inside the JSON string;
unescaping put a bare " in the middle of a string and the whole block failed
to parse. The shop then had no Store block and was dropped from the harvest.
Over the 839 cached pages: 113 blank before, 84 after, 29 shops recovered —
The Living Room, Fat Jim, Chiangmai 420, and HERBAL Garden Home: Cannabis and
Kratom, which is an eleventh kratom shop that was never reaching the site at
all. Parse first, unescape the values after; a page that only parses the old
way is still read that way. A second, nameless Store block on some pages could
also win the traversal, so the block must carry a name.

The harvest went 722 -> 755 shops. The extra 29 are cannabis records generally,
not kratom, and they land on the next import_all under the existing no-
automatic-dedupe rule.

PAI — KEPT, ON HER WORD, AND THE PIN WAS THE REAL PROBLEM
Nan, 2026-09-21: "keep the pai shops. People do end up in pai wondering
where's the weed and kratom." 49 shops in this harvest carry a Mae Hong Son
address and are filed cm, because weed.th's sitemap files Pai under its
chiang-mai path; 38 are already live in the cm corpus.

Keeping them turned out to mean fixing them, because every one was findable
only in the worst way: ALL 37 PAI SHOPS SAT ON ONE PIN 40.5 KM AWAY, in the
wrong district, tagged อ.กัลยาณิวัฒนา.

WHY. Postcode 58130 belongs to TWO districts in two provinces — อำเภอปาย in
Mae Hong Son and อำเภอกัลยาณิวัฒนา in Chiang Mai, which is the mountain
postal route and not a mistake. geocode_local's postcode tier is built on the
premise that "a five-digit Thai postcode names one delivery area and cannot be
confused", and our centroid for 58130 is the mean of sixteen Galyani Vadhana
schools and health stations. Every Pai address landed on it. The admin-polygon
pass then read that pin and stamped อ.กัลยาณิวัฒนา over a Mae Hong Son shop.

The tier already carries a guard for exactly this — "a postcode that
contradicts the named district is a typo" — and it could not fire, because it
asks whether the postcode disagrees with the district the address names, and
อำเภอปาย resolves to no district we hold. There was nothing to disagree with.

THREE FIXES, AND WHAT EACH IS FOR
1. geocode_local.stands_off_our_ground() — an address whose own postcode puts
   it outside CM and CR is refused before any tier runs. Pai now takes no pin
   rather than a confident wrong one, which is this module's own rule.

   IT READS THE POSTCODE, NOT THE PROVINCE NAME, and the first draft did the
   opposite and was wrong. Matching province names walks straight into the
   trap this module already documents for roads: พะเยา sits inside the
   building name สันพะเยาแมนชั่น, แพร่ inside the Hang Dong tambon น้ำแพร่ /
   Nam Phrae, and ลำพูน is a Chiang Mai road. Thai does not space its words,
   so there is no boundary to anchor to. That draft refused 158 addresses and
   most of them were ours. The first two digits of a postcode are a field and
   cannot be a road: 50 Chiang Mai, 57 Chiang Rai.

   Three things the reading itself had to learn, each measured rather than
   guessed: a decimal part is not a postcode (this field is pasted from map
   apps — "@18.79687,98.96510" offers two candidates and neither is one); the
   LAST candidate is the postcode, because a house number opens an address
   where a postcode closes it; and 99% of the 10,895 records whose confirmed
   postcode appears in their own address carry 20 characters or fewer after
   it, so a five-digit run with more address left than that is a house number.
   Thai postcodes run 10xxx–96xxx, which drops "ถนน hiway04301".

   A NAMED PROVINCE OVERRIDES, one way only: อ.กัลยาณิวัฒนา writes
   จ.เชียงใหม่ beside its 58130, so an address that names one of ours is kept
   whatever its postcode says.

   Refuses 56 of 49,142 addresses, 0.12%: 37 Pai, 9 Lamphun, and ten singles
   in Lampang, Phayao, Bangkok and one in Burbank, California. Around five are
   a store or house number misread as a postcode and lose a pin they had —
   the cost of the guard, against 37 records that were 40 km wrong, and in
   the direction this module already prefers.
2. derive.parse_address now reads a ROMANISED district — "Amphoe Pai",
   "Pai District". Every field in it was keyed on a Thai marker, so 47 of the
   49 Pai addresses parsed to a postcode and nothing else. Two traps found
   while writing it: a single-word capture read "Mae Taeng District" as Taeng
   and "Hang Dong" as Dong, a fragment of a real district and worse than no
   reading; and the Thai path let a province run on to the district, giving
   "ปาย แม่ฮ่องสอน" — the strip for that requires whitespace, because
   อำเภอเมืองเชียงใหม่ ends in its own province and must never be cut to
   เมือง.
3. attrs.amphoeSaid, the pair to the tambonSaid that already existed: a
   district the address names that this gazetteer cannot confirm. It never
   enters attrs.amphoe. build.py indexes it as a keyword, so a reader in Pai
   typing "pai" or "ปาย" reaches the shop. The area chip still needs a real
   area and does not show it.

MEASURED, BOTH PROVINCES: 37 Pai records now carry the district they are
actually in; ZERO confirmed amphoe changed anywhere; no fragments written.
