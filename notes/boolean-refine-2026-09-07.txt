1. WHAT YOU ASKED FOR

Three passes tonight, and the third is the one that ships:

  · "boolean tools to refine their own searches… intuitive for the krapow
     grannies"
  · "simpler, use fewer words, offer more refinements, without disturbing
     anyone's sabai"
  · "I want the boolean to look a lot more like Google's advanced search
     tools than this. This is hard to understand."
  · "find anything on motdang.net to a high degree of certainty and
     granularity… sufficient to HARD searches. But make an easy version
     available too."
  · "add sort/filter results"

The second pass produced a rail of chips ranked by how much each would split
the answer. It was clever, it used one chrome word, and you could not tell
what it was going to do. Clever is exactly what a boolean must not be. What
replaced it is the form Google has had since 2003, because anyone who has
used a computer has filled it in and nobody has ever had to be taught it.


2. THE TWO SURFACES

EASY — on the results page. The answer's count, one line saying what is
switched on, and a shut "เครื่องมือค้นหา · Search tools ▾". Open it and there
are six labelled dropdowns and two tickboxes: ประเภท · ย่าน · ถนน · มีอะไรบ้าง
· ชั้น · เรียงลำดับ, then เปิดอยู่ตอนนี้ and ใกล้ฉัน. Every option carries the
number of THESE results it would leave — counted on the answer in front of
you, not on the catalogue. Shut, it costs one line. A reader who got what they
wanted never opens it.

HARD — /advanced.html. The full form, in three labelled groups:

    หาที่มี…                      Find places with…
      มีทุกคำนี้                    all these words          (AND)
      วลีตรงตัวนี้                   this exact phrase
      มีคำใดคำหนึ่ง                  any of these words       (OR)
      ไม่มีคำเหล่านี้                  none of these words      (NOT)
      คำอยู่ตรงไหน                  where the words may match

    แล้วกรองให้แคบลง…             Then narrow by…
      ประเภท · ย่าน · ถนน · มีอะไรบ้าง · ชั้น   — pick as many as you like
      เปิดอยู่ตอนนี้ · ต้องมี · ยังไม่มี

    แล้วเรียงผลลัพธ์…              Then order the answer…
      เรียงลำดับ · ระยะทาง · ในระยะ

AND, OR and NOT ARE THE ROW LABELS. Nobody has to know that "มีทุกคำนี้" is
AND. They fill in the row that says what they want. That is the whole reason
Google's form has outlived every clever faceted-search UI built since.

The form is a real <form method="get">. It works with JavaScript off, and its
dropdowns are BAKED at build time with global counts, so the page is static
and instant and never fetches the index to show a list of districts.


3. WHAT MAKES IT SUFFICIENT FOR A HARD SEARCH

  WHERE THE WORDS MAY MATCH — the certainty control, and the one thing here
    that was not obvious. The index matches on seven fields, and two of them
    (`k`: shelf words, cuisine, brand, road · `a`: alt names, other-language
    names, the RTGS reading) are matched and NOT SHOWN. That is right for a
    wide search and wrong for somebody who knows the name of the shop they
    want. ชื่อเท่านั้น returns that shop and nothing standing near it.
  MULTIPLE VALUES PER FIELD — pick three districts and get all three (OR);
    pick a district and a trade and get the intersection (AND).
  ไม่เอา — an exclusion on any structured value, not just on words.
  ต้องมี / ยังไม่มี — presence and absence on the pin, the hours, the
    district, the road. "ยังไม่มี" is what the add and claim flows are for:
    working through a gap on purpose needs a way to ask for it.
  DISTANCE — the pin is on 97.7% of the catalogue, against hours at 17% and
    tags at 10%. It is the field we hold on nearly everything and it was the
    last one the search grew. ใกล้ฉัน + ในระยะ 500 ม. … 25 กม.
  SORT — ตรงที่สุด · ก-ฮ · ใกล้ที่สุด · ครบที่สุด. Only what the index can
    actually order on: there is no updatedAt in a row and no popularity
    anywhere, so neither is offered. ครบที่สุด counts how many of a row's
    fields are filled in, which is the plain stand-in for "the one we know
    most about".

WHAT IT STILL CANNOT DO, so you are not surprised by it: there is no phone,
website or price in the index rows, so none of the three can be filtered on.
Adding any of them is an index change, not a form change.

UNKNOWN IS NOT NO, throughout. An exclusion never removes a place for lacking
a field. A radius never removes a place with no pin — it is unmeasured, not
far away, and it sorts to the end rather than the front. เปิดอยู่ uses md.js's
own mdOpen, which returns null and never false where nobody recorded hours:
hand it over and the tickbox works, withhold it and the tickbox is disabled
rather than guessing. This is go/parking-bug written into the engine, where
unknown read as no cost 1,632 car parks their place in a parking search.

THE STATE IS THE URL. Every control is a real navigation, so the back button
is undo and any search can be sent to somebody on LINE. One line above the
answer says what is switched on, and every part of it is a tap that switches
it off.

5. THE DRIFT CHECK, AND WHAT IT CAUGHT

The one thing this needed and did not have. /advanced.html bakes its dropdown
counts in PYTHON at build time; the results page counts the same values in
JAVASCRIPT, live. Two readings of the same row, and nothing made them agree.
If they ever part, the form promises a number the answer will not honour —
you pick "สันติธรรม (148)" and get 143, with nothing on either page to say
why. Neither file would look wrong on its own.

So both readings now run over every row of the real catalogue and are compared
value for value, in order:

    the two readings agree on 359,101 row-and-key pairs across 88,161 records

They do agree, including on the awkward part — the five `has-phone`-style tags
that live in `t` but answer the "what it has" question and are counted under
`f` by both sides. `open` is excluded, and the exclusion is asserted rather
than assumed: it is answered against a clock, so no count baked at build time
could say so.

WHAT IT CAUGHT ON THE WAY. Four values in the catalogue carry an embedded
newline — a trade tag (กล่องอาหารเดลิเวอรี่ / บรรจุภัณฑ์อาหาร, one string),
two names, and one English name. Inside an <option> a browser draws that as a
gap or a second line, and the same text goes into title= and aria-label= where
it is read aloud as two things. The option LABEL is now collapsed to one line;
the `value=` keeps the exact string the filter matches on, so a tidied label
cannot select the wrong rows. A test proves the collapse still bites.

That is a display guard, not a fix. data/canonical is rewritten wholesale by
importers/import_all.py, so a hand edit there lasts until the next crawl. The
four records, for whoever owns the importers:

    cm-osm-node-13677628935     name      'Icon Park\nHotel Chiang Mai'
    cr-osm-way-401955473        nameEn    'Chiang Rai Passport Office\n(Immigration)'
    cm-curated-paper-41945      tradeTags 'กล่องอาหารเดลิเวอรี่\nบรรจุภัณฑ์อาหาร'
    (one further name; `python3 tests/test_refine.py` names them all)
