WO-78 — THE SECOND LINE
A result you can act on without opening it
2026-09-08


WHAT MICHAEL ASKED

  "Records that appear in search/map/discoverability functionality in Motdang
   should not require you to click them to see basic information (such as the
   lists displayed after search). You should be able to rank/sort results
   according to tags and your own matching criteria, without ever leaving the
   results page. Most of the details Motdang has could fit on a line or 2, and
   would cut down on clicks for human users (good--accessibility!). You need
   basic information about links in order to decide (as a human) whether
   clicking is worth the extra nav effort."


§1  WHAT THE ROW WAS THROWING AWAY

WO-69 (yesterday) made a result a card rather than a link: name, reading,
metres, an open-now lamp, three chips, a 📍. What it did not do is let anyone
FINISH on the list. Six things the record behind that row already held and the
row did not show:

    every tag it earned          `t`   — capped at ONE chip; 127 slugs exist
    its trade tags               `tt`  — never shown at all
    its street                   `st`  } the row showed whichever came first
    its tambon / amphoe          `ar`  } and dropped the other
    its opening schedule         `hk`  — spent on a lamp; the HOURS unread
    a phone / its own page       —     — not in the index at all

The first four were already ints on the index row. They were being computed,
shipped, and discarded at render time. Those cost nothing to put back.


§2  WHAT A ROW LOOKS LIKE NOW

Before:

    เดนทัล บลูม ●
    dentist · ฟอกสีฟัน · จัดฟัน                                        📍

After:

    เดนทัล บลูม ●
    dentist  จัดฟัน  รากฟันเทียม  ฟอกสีฟัน   เปิดถึง 22:00
    ☎ 052-007-389   facebook                                          📍

Order is the order a person scans in — what is it, where is it, when is it
open, how do I reach it. Every chip is a filter, so you can narrow by any tag
you can SEE, which until today was one tag per row. Past the fifth chip they
fold behind a +N that opens IN PLACE: no navigation, nothing dropped.

Measured across the whole catalogue (88,161 records):

    83%  will carry a phone or their own page on the row
    100% get a second line of some kind
    the ones that get NOTHING get nothing — a name-and-a-pin record renders no
         line at all rather than an empty bar. 63,170 of the records folded in
         this week are exactly that, and a row that draws a blank strip for
         each of them would be worse than the list we have.


§3  RANK AND SORT, ON THE RESULTS PAGE

The refine tools row (WO-75) already offers ตรงที่สุด · ก-ฮ · ใกล้ที่สุด ·
ครบที่สุด. It gains:

    ตรงแท็กมากสุด / most tags matched

which counts how many of the tag terms YOU asked for each row carries. With an
OR of two tags, the rows carrying both go on top. It only ever REORDERS — a row
carrying none of them scores nought and sorts last; a row nobody has tagged is
untagged, not disqualified. And ครบที่สุด now counts the phone and the page.


§4  THE MAP SIDE

A dot and a row are the same record seen twice, so the map card (WO-74) reads
the same two attributes and gains the same two things: the hours in words in
place of "open now", and the ☎ / page line. Map marks carry them.

Still not done on the map side, carried over from WO-74: event and merit marks,
and tag ints on the place-map neighbours and the explore/here layers, so chips
appear there too.


§5  WHAT IT COSTS

Two new index fields, both written in the shortest true form:

    ph   the phone, digits only, 60,921 rows              1.09 MB
    w    its own page, protocol-stripped, facebook.com/
         shortened to f/, 70,730 rows                     1.73 MB
                                                          -------
                                                          2.82 MB

NOT the address: 49,046 records hold one and it is 2.05 MB, and what it says is
the street and the district — which the row already carries as two chips at a
fortieth of the weight.

`importers/apply_rowline_hook.py --no-index` leaves both out. The line still
draws the tags, the place, the hours and the fold; it loses the ☎ and the link,
which is the 83%.


§6  THE INDEX, AND A CEILING THAT IS GONE

An earlier draft of this note deferred to a 10,000,000-byte budget in
tests/test_index_weight.py as though it had standing. It did not. Asked where
the number came from, the answer was: a session wrote it on 2026-09-07 at
13:28, it was never committed to git, and the file's own docstring says the
number is "what index.json weighed that afternoon, plus the cost of that day's
change, rounded up." A ratchet against Sunday, not a judgment about a reader.
Nan, 2026-09-08: "the 10 mb ceiling is a hallucination that needs nuked."

It is nuked. test_index_weight.py now REPORTS weight — raw and gzipped — and
fails only on the things that are correctness rather than taste: a relation
int that points outside its table (a chip that renders as nothing or as the
wrong place's name, which no reader could ever catch), the unread `h` string
creeping back, a pinned row with no shelf.

The real cost was never a raw byte count on one file. It is what a phone
fetches before the first character of an answer, and Nan on the same day:
"1.5 mb before an answer is a problem that needs fixed." Measured against the
LIVE index this morning, and against what the Overture fold does to it:

    live today, 25,582 rows                 1.56 MB gzipped
    after the fold, 89,082 rows, unchanged  6.50 MB gzipped

WO-78's 2.82 MB raw sits on top of that. It is a real cost and it is not the
problem; the problem is a whole catalogue shipped to answer one question about
one street corner, and it is about to be four times worse. That is its own
work order, opened, and the numbers are in §9.

§9  THE 1.5 MB — WHERE IT ACTUALLY GOES

Measured on the live index this morning (25,582 rows, 1,563,679 gzipped), by
stripping each field and re-measuring. No single fat field; it is spread:

    n   name        271,899  17%     s   slug        220,469  14%
    k   keywords    246,780  16%     lat+lng         264,442  17%
    id              102,244   7%     e   English      98,657   6%
    a   aliases      52,063   3%     everything else 306,000  20%

Two levels of fix:

  CHEAP, no architecture change — 196 KB (13%)
    coordinates to 5 dp (1.1 m, which is finer than any pin we hold)  64 KB
    drop `id`, use the array position                                102 KB
    drop `pv`, derivable from `p`                                     30 KB

  THE SPLIT — 42%
    Of the fields on a row, thirteen are needed only to DRAW a row that is
    actually shown: s, lat, lng, st, ar, hk, f, fx, r, nm, em, pv, ob. The
    reader sees two hundred rows and we ship twenty-five thousand rows' worth
    of them. Split in two, the first answer costs 941 KB instead of 1,564 KB;
    with the cheap wins, 774 KB — half of today.

  AND IT IS STILL NOT ENOUGH. Half of 6.50 MB is 3.2 MB. The structural
  answer is that /api/v1/places is deployed, answers in about 120 ms, and the
  search box has never called it — so a first answer could cost no index at
  all. That is the front-door work, and it trades offline-first for it, which
  is a decision about what this site IS and is not mine.
