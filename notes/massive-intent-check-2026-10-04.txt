5. THAI SEGMENTATION: SPACED vs NO-SPACE
  th-TH as published (spaced):   12.1% fired
  th-TH spaces removed:          8.6% fired
  en-US for reference:           18.0% fired
  MASSIVE annotators often typed spaces between Thai words ('จอง ตั๋ว รถไฟ'), which a search-box user does not.
  The spaced number overstates what real Thai input gets. Per-intent no-space numbers are in the thNS% column of section 2.
  Our ids lost when spaces go: weather:178, contact:123, best:71, weekend:55, buy:43, book:38, learn:34, early:32, sunday:29, near:25, price:21, kids:20
  Our ids gained when spaces go: howto:45, near:24, weather:10, route:9, delivery:2, early:2, late:1, open:1
  Gains are front patterns that now have a Thai char after them (อะไรคือ, เส้นทางไป) and near ใกล้ฉัน, which in MASSIVE is often
  written 'ใกล้ ฉัน'. Losses are patterns that sat at a space boundary and now sit mid-run.

  Simulation (not a code change): if every Thai pattern of 5+ code points also matched anywhere in a run (short ones, and 18 known
  ambiguous ones keep the end-of-run rule), no-space coverage would go from 8.6% to 11.9%.
  The patterns that gain most: สภาพอากาศ, พยากรณ์อากาศ, ฝนตก(ไหม), จองตั๋ว, แนะนำ, แถวนี้, สุดสัปดาห์, ทางไป/เส้นทางไป.
  The cost is the tail-collision class above for any pattern that is also the end of another word, so a per-pattern
  'not after' list is needed either way (กำแพง, อู/แฮม/ชีส+เบอร์, ออน+ไลน์, ร้อง/นัก/โรง+เรียน, ซี/ดี+เจ, ดูด+ฝุ่น, ผู้/รายชื่อ+ติดต่อ).
