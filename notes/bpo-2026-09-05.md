## The measurement

Across 22,805 records in both provinces, **five names** say anything about
outsourcing, call centres, accounting-as-a-service or recruitment — and two
of those five are a hospital and a government audit office matched on the
word เซ็นเตอร์ / บัญชี. The business shelf held 30 coworking, 17 wholesale
and 12 professional records and nothing that names this trade.

The reason is the WO-9 naming custom again, one layer out: **half of this
city's BPO work is in Thai and calls itself สำนักงานบัญชี (an accounting
office) or รับทำบัญชี (bookkeeping accepted), never BPO**; the other half
is farang-facing and calls itself an outsourcing company in English on a
site the crawl never reads.

## What was read (verify-before-believe, `cache/lens/bpo/`)

**CLBS — Chiangmai Lanna Business Services Ltd**, 92/1 Mahidol Road, Haiya,
+66 53 90 47 48, info@clbs.co.th. Its own site: an international outsourcing
company based in Chiang Mai doing **inbound customer service in the client's
own language** — answering calls, scheduling appointments, managing orders —
for small businesses and freelancers across Europe. Hiring German, French,
Spanish, Dutch and English speakers **with visa, work permit and health
insurance**. And the line that matters most for Nan's project: it states
**"promoted by the Board of Investment"**.

**SANS Chiang Mai**, 053-245-473. Its own site: company registration,
accounting and payroll, visas and work permits, **BOI accounting**, Xero and
QuickBooks training, personal income tax, HR recruitment — as a package or à
la carte. This is the back-office stack a new shop buys rather than builds.

**PRTR** — large Thai staffing and outsourcing group, offices in Bangkok
(Ital-Thai Tower) and Sriracha only. **No Chiang Mai office on the page
read**, so no record; written into the register so nobody adds one from a
search result.

**The Thai staffing market** serves Chiang Mai from Bangkok: ROYS, SO PEOPLE
and the JOBBKK contract-type filter "สัญญาจ้างภายใต้บริษัท Outsource" all
state call-centre and admin placement without an office here. That absence is
the answer, not a silence.

## The three registers a reader can check for themselves

- **BOI** (boi.go.th) — the route by which a foreign-owned service company
  may hold a majority the Foreign Business Act would otherwise refuse. CLBS
  states its own promotion, which is evidence the route works here. 2026
  reporting says digital activities are now one heading with an 8-year CIT
  exemption and a minimum investment computed from Thai IT staff salaries.
  **The BOI site would not answer an automated fetch** — everything here is
  a company's claim or press, and the page says which.
- **DBD data warehouse** — search a company by name or 13-digit number and
  read its filed accounts. **403 to a plain fetch**; open it in a browser.
- **ประกันสังคม** — a real job leaves a social-security record and a payslip.
