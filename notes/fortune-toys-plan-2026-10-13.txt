ENRICH, by toy
Siamsi
- 28 sticks to the full 100 of the temple sets, one set per tradition (Chinese temple,
  Thai wat). Each stick: verse, plain reading, five asks (work, money, love, health,
  travel), a verdict.
- The cylinder: shake on the phone's motion sensor, a stick rises, a shaken sound.
- Place pairing: each set links to the wats and shrines on the map that keep that tradition.
- Print: a stick as a paper slip, Thai on one side, English on the other.
Horoscope
- Birth date once, kept in the browser; the day page opens to that sign.
- Week and month ahead, from the same arithmetic.
- Auspicious days: wan phra, lucky hours by day-of-week colour, the Thai calendar.
- Compatibility: two birth dates, three systems side by side.
8-ball
- Answers grouped by kind of question (yes/no, when, which road), 60+ lines.
- The ask box reads the question and picks from the kind.
- Shake on motion; the face rises in the water. Each answer cites a Chiang Mai place when it fits.
All

- Share card: today's reading as a 1200x630 image.
- Reading log (plain tsv): toy, answer id, thumbs. Counts which answers people keep.
