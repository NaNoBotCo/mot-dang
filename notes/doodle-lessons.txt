HOW PEOPLE ACT (from the 2026-10-12 word; add what drawings teach)

Every person has a reason to be in the frame and is doing it: selling, haggling, carrying, eating, riding, waiting, phoning, chatting, praying, minding a child. Two or three of them relate to each other (look at, hand over, laugh with). Poses differ; timing differs; no two people move in step unless the subject is a dance or a procession. Thai everyday ways: the wai, sitting on heels or a low stool, a scooter with a passenger side-saddle, an umbrella against the sun, shopkeepers resting at the counter. The kit's person() adds breath and weight shift at rest (doodler.js natural()); a scene still owes each figure an action.
