THE ANSWER FIRST
================

Yes. And four fifths of it is enrichment, not new building — the records
are already on disk, the print rig already exists, and the one thing that
is genuinely missing is the one that matters most: the site does not work
without a network.

WHAT IS MISSING, IN THE ORDER IT WOULD HURT
===========================================

1.  NO OFFLINE. Zero service workers across 23,434 pages. In a flood or
    a quake the network is the first thing to go and the site goes with
    it. Everything else on this list is worth less than this one.

2.  THE FOUR NUMBERS ARE ON ONE SHELF. Not on the homepage, not on a
    page of their own, not on the app, not on paper. Somebody who lands
    on a noodle shop page at 3am has no path to 1669.

3.  NO PHONE ON 95% OF MEDICAL ROWS. 92 of 1,946 medical records carry a
    phone number ON THIS DISK. (Not a claim that the numbers do not
    exist — a claim about what this repo holds.)

4.  NO HAZARD LAYER AT ALL. No flood, no smoke shelter, no shelter or
    evacuation point, no "which hospital has an ER open tonight".

The season is now: the Ping runs high September–October. Smoke is
February–April. The March 2025 Mandalay quake was felt through the city.
Three hazards, three different answers, one city.


THE RULE THAT SHAPES ALL OF IT
==============================

This site says WHERE THINGS ARE AND WHAT THEY ARE. It is not a clinician
and it is not ปภ. So the layer is locations, numbers, hours, elevations
and official sources — never triage, never "what to do if", never a
prediction. emergency.json already says this in its own _rule and the
rule holds here.

Second rule, from WO-52: an invitation that has never been answered is
furniture. So no "report flooding here" box. The channel that works is
the one nobody advertises.

Third: a shelter list that is wrong at 3am is worse than no list. Any
shelter or evacuation data comes from a named official announcement with
a date, or it does not go up.


THE FORK — pick by number, any combination
==========================================

1 · OFFLINE FIRST — a service worker and a pocket page
------------------------------------------------------
    One page, /chuai.html (ช่วย · help), that caches itself: the four
    numbers, the nearest ER hospitals with their pins, pharmacies open
    now, fuel, water points, and the city's own emergency accounts. Once
    a reader has opened it once, it answers with the network down.
    Plus a service worker that keeps the last-visited pages readable.

    Cost:      ~1 day. Stdlib only, no new dependency.
    Worst case: a stale cache shows yesterday's opening hours. Mitigated
               by stamping every cached page with its build date.
    Undo:      delete one file; the site is static and unchanged.

2 · THE NUMBERS EVERYWHERE — 30 minutes
----------------------------------------
    Move the emergency band out of the medical shelf: footer of every
    page as a single tel: row, and a full card on /chuai.html. Four
    tel: links, no prose. This is the cheapest item on the list and the
    one most likely to matter.

    Cost:      under an hour.
    Worst case: four more link-instances per page. Measured against the
               WO-52 word count before it ships; if it fails the count
               it goes in the footer once and nowhere else.
    Undo:      one function.

3 · THE PAPER SHEET — A4, Thai first, fridge-door
--------------------------------------------------
    Reuse make_handouts.py exactly as it stands. One sheet: the four
    numbers at the size you can read across a room, the nearest hospital
    per amphoe, PM2.5 bands with what the colours mean, and the QR to
    /chuai.html. Black only. Photocopies. Works with no power.
    Trilingual is on the table — Thai, English, Shan — with the same
    UNVERIFIED banner rule the housekeeper sheet already carries.

    Cost:      half a day, no new code, needs Chrome.
    Worst case: printed numbers go stale. Only nationally-issued numbers
               go on paper, exactly as emergency.json already requires.
    Undo:      it is a PDF.

4 · SMOKE SEASON — the clean-air layer  (build now, matters February)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
    air.json already holds the readings and the PCD bands. What is
    missing is WHERE TO GO: the malls, libraries, hospitals and offices
    with filtered air, drawn from records already on disk, plus the
    government clean-air rooms (ห้องปลอดฝุ่น) when an official list
    with a date can be found.

    Cost:      1–2 days, most of it verification, not code.
    Worst case: a room listed as clean-air is not. Mitigated by only
               listing rooms an official source names, with the date.
    Undo:      one layer file.

5 · THE FLOOD LAYER — and the honest limit
-------------------------------------------
    What this repo can truthfully do: show each pin's ground elevation
    from SRTM and its distance from the Ping, and put the P.1 gauge
    reading (Nawarat Bridge, กรมชลประทาน) next to the levels at which
    the city has historically taken water.

    What it CANNOT do: tell anyone whether their house floods. SRTM at
    z12 is roughly 38 m per cell with 5–10 m of vertical error. That is
    a terrain profile, not a flood model, and any page built on it must
    say so in its first line or it is lying.

    Cost:      2 days, and one open question — whether the P.1 gauge has
               a fetchable feed. Unknown; would need checking.
    Worst case: false confidence. This is the item I would build LAST,
               and only with the limit printed at the top.
    Undo:      one layer file.

6 · ENRICH WHAT IS ALREADY THERE — the unglamorous one
-------------------------------------------------------
    · phones onto the 124 hospitals and 469 health stations
    · which hospitals run a 24-hour ER, from their own stated hours
    · the water points already fetched and never built
    · fuel + 24h convenience already have hours; surface them on
      /chuai.html as "open now" instead of a separate shelf

    Cost:      2–3 days, mostly reading sources.
    Worst case: a wrong phone number. Every one carries its source and
               fetch date, same as every other record on this site.
    Undo:      per-record.


EXPIRY
======

Items 1, 2, 3 and 6 have no expiry — they are better the day before
they are needed and useless the day after.

Item 4 has one: it is worth building before February. After the smoke
starts it is a page written in a hurry.

Item 5 has one too, the other way: the Ping is high NOW, Sept–Oct. If
the flood layer is wanted for this season it needs the go inside two
weeks. Otherwise it waits for next year, which is fine.


WHAT I DID NOT DECIDE
=====================

Whether ANY of this ships. This is a proposal with numbers attached,
not a plan, and nothing is held or holstered — it is simply not built.
Say a number, a set of numbers, or no.
