MOT DANG — WHY THE SITE IS FRUSTRATING TO USE
Diagnosis, 2026-09-07, after Nan's phone test of motdang.net
"For 1-2 results, you shouldn't have to long-scroll. Never."
"Maybe search isn't even the right tool."
"Human searches will always be inexact... We should be anticipating user
 intent better throughout. And not showing extraneous anything."

Everything below is measured, not asserted. Where a number is from the live
site it says so; where it is from the fixed-but-undeployed build it says so.

==============================================================================
1. THE PAGE IS 98% FURNITURE
==============================================================================

One-result search ("rajavej hospital"), 375 × 812 phone, measured in the page:

    the answer                27 px
    the whole document     1,484 px
    the answer is           1.8% of it

    what the other 1,457 px are:
      site header + language buttons + search box   140 px
      the shortcut grid (map, plan, my page, …)     344 px
      the services bar                               26 px
      the footer                                    558 px

Your rule — for 1-2 results you should never have to long-scroll — is not
broken by the results. It is broken by everything wrapped around them. The
grid and the services bar are already MOVED below the results when there is a
query, which was somebody's fix for this, and it is not enough: they still sit
between the answer and the end of the page, and the footer is another 558 px.

For a one-result answer the page should end after the answer.

25,179 rows — the ENTIRE catalogue of both provinces — to answer one question

Every question, however small, pays for every place we hold.

==============================================================================
3. THE TAP AFTER A RESULT USUALLY REVEALS NOTHING
==============================================================================

A result row is a link. The link costs another page — ~47 KB, ~12.5 KB
gzipped. What is on the other side, for the 1,796 car parks:

    have a phone number             0
    have opening hours              2
    say whether they cost         163   (9%)
    say who may park there        303   (17%)
    say nothing beyond the pin  1,214   (68%)

So for two thirds of them the row already contains everything we know, and
the link is a promise of more that is not there. A reader taps, waits, reads
the same name back, and taps return. Three actions, no information.

==============================================================================
4. THE SAME QUESTION, NINE WAYS, NINE DIFFERENT ANSWERS
==============================================================================

One intent — where do I leave a motorbike near Tha Phae Gate — put to the
fixed build in the ways a person actually types it:

  query                                              found   first result
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  parking thapae gate                                1,796   car park,   80 m  ✅
  somewhere to park near tha phae                    1,981   car park,  100 m  ✅
  จอดรถ ท่าแพ                                            38   street-side,460 m  ~
  Motorcycle parking near taphae   (what you typed)    134   moto bay,  810 m  ~
  motorcyle parking taphae         (a typo)            134   moto bay,  810 m  ~
  ที่จอดมอเตอร์ไซค์ ท่าแพ                                133   moto bay,  810 m  ~
  where can i park my motorbike near tha phae gate       9   a lot named
                                                             ชาวสวนยางพารา    ✗
  park my bike tha phae                                 20   "Bike Park", 6 km ✗
  moto parking taphae                                    1   a shop,     4.6 km ✗

The nearest car park to Tha Phae Gate is 80 metres away. Four of these nine
phrasings never show it. One returns a single shop 4.6 km away. The reader has
no way to know which phrasing is the magic one, and no reason to believe the
site holds anything better than what it just showed them.

This is the systemic fault, and §5 is why it happens.

==============================================================================
5. THE SEVEN CAUSES
==============================================================================

CAUSE 1 — THE WORDS ARE MATCHED, THE INTENT IS NOT.
  "motorcycle" is compared as TEXT against names. Rows literally named
  ที่จอดมอเตอร์ไซค์ match; the car park 80 m away does not, because its name
  does not contain the word. **Asking the narrower question returns the worse
  answer.** A person who says "motorbike" has told us something useful and we
  punish them for it.

CAUSE 2 — A VOCABULARY GAP FAILS SILENTLY.
  "moto" is not in the thesaurus, so "moto parking taphae" matched one shop
  and reported it with total confidence. Nothing on the page says "we did not
  understand one of your words." Compare "parking", which IS understood.

CAUSE 3 — EXTRA WORDS ARE TREATED AS REQUIREMENTS.
  "where can i park my motorbike near tha phae gate" has eleven words. The
  engine wants rows matching all of them, fails, and falls back to whatever
  matched most — nine odd rows. A sentence is punished for being a sentence,
  which is how humans ask.

CAUSE 4 — THE COUNT IS THE HEADLINE, AND IT ANSWERS NOTHING.
  "Search results 134". For "where is the nearest X" the honest headline is
  the first one, its distance, and whether it costs. 134 is a boast about the
  catalogue, not an answer to the question.

CAUSE 5 — THE ROWS ARE IDENTICAL, AND THAT ONE IS MINE.
  1,796 car parks carry 1,039 distinct names; 87 of them read exactly
  "ที่จอดรถ · ย่านแม่แตง-เชียงดาว". Under a distance sort I strip the bearing
  off the name so the distance is not printed twice — which leaves 134 rows
  all reading "ที่จอดมอเตอร์ไซค์ Motorcycle parking", differing only in the
  metres. I turned a wall of elephants into a wall of identical car parks.
  A row has to carry the thing that tells it from its neighbour: the street,
  the landmark, the fee, the capacity — whichever we actually hold.

CAUSE 6 — THE DEFAULT LANGUAGE MODE PRINTS EVERYTHING TWICE.
  ไทย + EN is the default. 6,788 rows (26%) carry an English name distinct
  from the Thai and print both. My distance chip renders "810 ม. · 810 m".
  Every row is longer than it needs to be for a reader who reads either
  language, and on a phone that is the difference between three answers on
  screen and six.

CAUSE 7 — THE PAGE HAS NO IDEA HOW MANY ANSWERS IT IS SHOWING.
  It lays out 1 result and 200 results identically: same header, same
  furniture, same footer, same 200-row cap. Nothing anywhere asks "is this a
  short answer or a long list", which is the question that decides the whole
  layout.

==============================================================================
6. "MAYBE SEARCH ISN'T EVEN THE RIGHT TOOL." — NO, IT IS NOT
==============================================================================

For this class of question you are right, and the evidence is in the data.

"Where can I park near Tha Phae Gate" is not a text query. It is three
structured facts:

    WHAT    a kind of place        (parking, and it has a shelf)
    WHERE   a point                (a landmark, and we hold 68 of them)
    HOW FAR a radius               (walking distance, unstated but obvious)

A text box captures none of the three. It gets a string, and we spend the
whole pipeline trying to recover the three facts from it — which is exactly
what WO-68 started doing (lift the landmark out, lift the constraint out) and
why the good phrasings suddenly worked. The lesson of that work is not "the
search got better", it is **the search only works when it stops being a
search.**

And the site can already do this. 24,019 of 25,179 records (95%) carry a pin.
The instruments exist: the map, "ห้องน้ำใกล้ฉัน · Toilets near you", "open
now", "plan a route". The one thing missing is that the search box does not
know to hand the question over to them.

So: keep the box — it is where people start, and it should be. Change what it
DOES with a where-question. A query that resolves to a kind and a place should
not render a list of 134 links; it should render the answer:

    ที่จอดรถ ใกล้ประตูท่าแพ · Parking near Tha Phae Gate

    ลานจอดรถ            100 ม.   ฟรี · free · 18 คัน
    ที่จอดรถ             80 ม.   ลูกค้าเท่านั้น · customers only
    อาคารจอดรถ          220 ม.   อาคาร · multi-storey
                                       [ แผนที่ · on the map ]
    ทั้งหมด 1,796 แห่ง · all 1,796

Three answers, the fact that decides between them, and a map. No 200 rows, no
shelf headings, no count as a headline, and nothing below it.

==============================================================================
7. WHAT I PROPOSE — SYSTEMIC, IN THE ORDER THAT PAYS
==============================================================================

P1. THE PAGE FITS THE ANSWER.  (small, and it is your standing rule)
    Under about four results, nothing renders below them — the shortcut grid,
    the services bar and the footer all sit out. One result should be one
    screen with no scroll at all. Today it is 27 px of answer in 1,484 px.

P2. ONE LANGUAGE PER ROW.  (small)
    In ไทย+EN the row keeps both NAMES, because the two names are two facts,
    but never doubles a unit or a computed word: "810 ม." or "810 m", not
    both. Cuts every distance-sorted row by a third.

P3. A ROW CARRIES WHAT TELLS IT APART.  (medium — undoes Cause 5, which I made)
    Fee, capacity, structure, access, the street — whichever we hold, in the
    row, chosen by what the reader asked. A list of identical names is not a
    list.

P4. INTENT BEFORE WORDS.  (medium, and this is the systemic one)
    Resolve the query to (kind, place, constraints) FIRST — the landmark lift
    and the constraint lift already do two thirds of this — then use the
    leftover words as a name filter rather than as requirements. Effects:
      · a sentence stops being punished for its extra words;
      · "motorbike" ranks dedicated bays first and STILL shows the car park
        80 m away, instead of hiding it;
      · a word we did not understand is SAID, not swallowed.

P5. THE WHERE-QUESTION GETS THE WHERE-ANSWER.  (larger)
    When kind + place both resolve, render the short answer above, not a
    results list. This is where "search isn't the right tool" becomes code.

P6. STOP SHIPPING THE CATALOGUE TO ANSWER ONE QUESTION.  (larger)
    1.5 MB before the first character. A per-shelf index, or a small
    name-and-pin index with the rest fetched on demand, is the difference
    between this site being usable on Thai mobile data and not.

P7. SAY WHEN THE ANSWER IS THIN.
    2 of 1,796 car parks have opening hours; 0 have a phone. A reader
    deciding between two lots deserves to be told that nobody has recorded
    which is open, rather than tapping through to find out twice.
