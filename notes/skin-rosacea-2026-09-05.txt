THE ANSWER (for a reader who asks "who does rosacea in Chiang Mai")

No sign in either province names rosacea, or any skin disease. It goes
through one door — a dermatology clinic — and eight hospitals state one on
their own pages. Their words, their hours:

Chiang Mai
  Suan Dok (Maharaj) — CMU Division of Dermatology: skin clinic Tue/Thu/Fri
      09:00–12:00, floor 10 Sriphat building; laser clinic Thu 13:00–15:00;
      UV phototherapy room floor 12 all weekdays. Division office 053-935482-3.
      The only state clinic in the north that prints its days.
  Sriphat Medical Center — skin & aesthetic clinic, floor 5 Chalermprakiat
      building, 053-934733. Same medical school, the self-pay door.
  Chiangmai Ram — skin & aesthetic centre, Mon–Fri 08–20, Sat 08–17,
      Sun 08–16, 052-004622. Its page separates "disease" from "complexion".
  Bangkok Hospital Chiang Mai — skin clinic, Bangkok Plaza floor 1,
      daily 08:00–16:30, 052-089-846 / 1719. Lists psoriasis, dermatitis,
      urticaria, skin cancer by name.
  Rajavej — "skin disease" Mon–Thu 09:00–11:00; "health & complexion"
