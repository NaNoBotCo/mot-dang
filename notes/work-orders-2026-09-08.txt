§4  WHAT I DID NOT RENUMBER, AND WHY

The tag mine and one of the two WO-77s should move. I did not move them.

Every one of their references lives in files two other sessions are writing
this morning — build.py, refine_layer.py, glyphs.py, nownear_layer.py — while
a deploy has been running for half an hour. Renaming another session's work
order inside a file they have open is how you get a half-renamed tree and a
lost edit, and the session that owns the work is the one that should pick its
own number.

So this register does the job the renaming would have done: every existing
reference is resolvable by reading it. Nothing is broken, nothing of anyone
else's was touched, and the ambiguity in your decision is gone.
