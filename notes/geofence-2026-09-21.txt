==============================================================================
THE ONE NUMBER THAT SHAPED BOTH
==============================================================================

  Cloudflare told the site where this connection was, and it was wrong by
  6 km. It said 18.790, 98.985 — Chiang Mai, near the old city. The house it
  was sent from is at 18.789, 99.045. Measured today, straight off the live
  edge.

  So the network's guess is a CITY, not a person. It is enough to put you in
  Chiang Mai rather than Chiang Rai, enough to pick pictures near you, enough
  to sort a list. It is not enough to print "2.4 km" at anybody.

  Both halves below therefore draw the same line twice:

    the network's guess   "ใกล้ๆ · nearby" under 1.5 km, "~6 km" above,
                          and the page says whose guess it is
    your own position     "400 m", "6.4 km" — a real number, on a tap

==============================================================================
1. THE GEOFENCE now reaches every page
==============================================================================

  Before: one page — the front page — carried the meta the edge fills in.
  Now: all of them. Nothing was rebuilt and nothing was re-uploaded; the
  Worker fills it as the page streams past.

  And a page that says where IT is now says how far that is from you. Under
  the heading of any place page:

      ~6 กม. · ~6 km ตะวันออก · E — จากตำแหน่งโดยประมาณของเครือข่าย
      Chiang Mai · from your network's rough position

  It works with JavaScript off, because the Worker writes it, not the page.
  It costs no extra fetch: the coordinate is already in the page's head, in
  the schema.org block, and the heading comes after it — one pass.

  WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT DONE: a directory page that lists a hundred places
  carries a hundred coordinates and is not standing at any of them, so it
  gets no line. The hub pages over 2 MB are skipped entirely — walking a
  68 MB page to learn it is a list is not worth the time.

  A page carrying your city is marked private, so no shared cache can hold
  one reader's copy for the next.

==============================================================================
2. DISTANCE IN SEARCH
==============================================================================

  76,847 of the 105,813 pages in the index — 73% — now carry their own
  coordinate, read from what each page publishes about itself.

  On the results page:

    เรียง · Sort now offers ใกล้ที่สุด · Nearest, and it appears only when
    the site knows where you are.

    Every result that has a pin carries its distance and direction, in the
    rounding the position has earned.

    A 📍 button asks your browser for your real position and reloads the same
    address with it. That button is the only script the search page carries,
    and it is written only when a distance is on offer.

  A page with no coordinate is UNMEASURED, not far: it sorts last, and it is
  never dropped from an answer.

  Distance is computed with longitude scaled by the cosine of the latitude,
  so a place due east is not ranked closer than one the same distance north.
  Over the whole province the error against the true curve is under half a
  percent; even to Bangkok it is 583 km against 585.

TWO THINGS YOU SHOULD KNOW
==============================================================================

  1. THE FRONT PAGE YOU TESTED IS NOT THE PAGE THAT IS LIVE.

     The front page was rebuilt at 16:18 today and the routine site sync
     carried it out at 17:54. The new one has no geofence: no position, no
     pictures that follow you, no NEAR. The queue still lists that page as
     HELD on a red gate — the build-and-publish path took it live anyway,
     which is worth knowing on its own.

     The 2026-09-19 page is saved whole, and putting it back is one command.
     The choice is in the queue as C82. I did not make it: the front page is
     yours.

     What I did do: the position now arrives on every page from the Worker,
     written into the head when the page itself carries no tag. So whichever
     front page you keep, it has a position to work from.

  2. THE PINS ARE 3,705 OF 76,847, AND CLOUDFLARE IS WHY.

     Loading the coordinates needs D1's import endpoint, and this afternoon
     it answered 502 Bad Gateway and then locked the whole database —
     every query, not just the import — and took motdang.net/find down for
     25 minutes. A chunk that had taken 17 seconds was still running after
     ten minutes. I stopped it, the search came straight back, and I left
     the rest unloaded rather than trade a working search for coordinates.

     Distance therefore works today for the pages that have a coordinate and
     is simply absent for the others — unmeasured is not far, the same rule
     the rest of the search keeps.

     Finishing is one command and about ten minutes, and it will make the
     search blink while it runs. Worth doing at a quiet hour, not at seven
     in the evening.

     One lasting change came out of it: a reload now writes over the live
     rows instead of building a second copy and swapping. The old way held
     two 660 MB indexes at once, which is the state the database was in when
     it fell over.
